## I. Kenseier (class 22, `kenseier`; the ashen parry tank): 6 ability icons, 2 spec moves, 2 talent moves, 1 Ultimate, 3 portraits

The art direction: a quiet master of the blade who answers every blow with a deflect, never a flashy samurai and never a demon-slayer. Everything is **ash grey, charcoal and pale steel**, with **one ember-orange accent (`#ff8a3d`)** on the cutting edge of the blade and wherever a blow is turned, and **white-gold (`#ffe2a0`) used only for the boss's Posture bar and a Break** (so the gold always means the payoff). The weapon is the **ashblade**: a long, slightly curved, single-edged blade, pale steel with a thin flat ember-orange stripe along the cutting edge, a round charcoal guard, a gold collar and a grip wrapped in ash-grey cord. The motifs are *the blade* (the class's object), *the spark* (a flat white-orange starburst where steel meets a blow, with a thin ring round it), *the haori* (an ash-grey open coat, pleated, with ember-orange piping) and *the beat* (a row of evenly spaced beads or a closing ring). Damage is a single clean ember cut, never a slash cloud; blows turned aside are sparks, never explosions. **The only red allowed is the telegraph stripe on Mikiri Step (`#c43a30`)**, because red means damage in this game. Each icon has a different silhouette inside the class so the hotbar reads in grey: a diagonal sweeping cut (Ember Cut), a blade meeting a claw in a spark (Deflect), footprints across a stripe (Mikiri Step), a blade through a split bar (Deathblow), a hanging coat (Ash Mantle), a blade over a sheltered figure (Guard Stance), a circle of crossing slashes (Flash of Ash). Use the same negative prompt as above and add "samurai armour, ninja, oni mask, cherry blossom, red sun, kanji, dragon, katana in a sheath on a rack, blood" to it for this class. Files go in `client/icons/kenseier/<id>.png` (the game draws its own placeholders until they arrive; `art.icons` is `false` for the class in `data.js` until the set is complete). The taunt rides on Ember Cut and reuses its icon (`cut`), so there is no taunt icon. The two spec moves and the two talent moves have their own ids (`iai`, `redirect`, `whirlwind`, `duel`; the kit borrows Guard Stance's, Deathblow's and Mikiri Step's art for them until they exist, so set `icon:` on each in `kits.js` / `specs.js` once the PNGs are in): add `"whirlwind"` and `"duel"` to `client/icons/talent-icons.json` too. The Ultimate's art is `thousandcuts` (the kit borrows `deathblow`'s until then). Do NOT add anything to `client/items/manifest.json`.
