# Hero portrait briefs (hero select and inventory)

**Format:** square head-and-shoulders portraits (a "bust"), about 512×512 each.
- The face sits a little above the center, with the top of the head or headgear just inside the frame.
- Show down to about mid-chest. Props (staff head, hourglass, rune stones) may peek in at the sides.
- Facing the viewer or a slight three-quarter turn; same painted style and lighting as the ability and item icons.
- A soft rim light in the hero's accent color, on a plain dark background with a faint glow in that color.
- No frame, border or text: the game frames them.

**Sheet:** a 3×3 grid of equal squares in this order is ideal. Keep the same character designs as the current sheet so they stay recognizable.
