# Boss VFX reference sheets (one per boss)

2026-10-20. The class sheets you painted (`docs/art/vfx-reference/`) set the look for player abilities: a small wind-up, a bold main shape (hot white core, saturated mid colour, dark ink edges), then a slow aftermath (smoke, embers, decals, debris). These four sheets do the same for the bosses. I draw everything in code, so what helps most is **shape, colour and timing**, not finish. A clear, flat, painted sketch beats a polished render.

## What a boss effect needs that a player effect doesn't

- **The warning.** Every boss hit is shown on the floor before it lands, and never hidden. The warning is the most important frame: it has to read at a glance, in a crowd, under other effects, as *that* move and no other.
- **The boss's own tell.** What his body does during the cast (Grubnak raises the cleaver, Volkar draws breath). It should be readable from across the room.
- **The landing.** Bigger and heavier than any player effect: it should feel like it hurts.
- **What stays.** Pools, cracks, rubble, scorch, silk: anything that stays on the floor and changes where you can stand.

## Shared style (paste this before every sheet)

> A game VFX concept sheet in a clean, hand-painted style, like a stylised fantasy RPG effects sheet. A top-down three-quarter view, about 50° from overhead, the same angle as the game. Each effect is shown in **three panels left to right: the warning (the floor marking and the boss's tell), the impact (the bold main shape: a hot white-yellow core, a saturated mid colour, dark inky outer edges), and the aftermath (what's left on the floor for a few seconds).** A small grey hero silhouette in each panel for scale. Flat, readable shapes with hard edges; soft glow only at the very core. A plain, dark, neutral background (slate grey), so the colours read. A short handwritten label under each row is fine.

**Avoid:** photoreal smoke, lens flare, bloom over everything, thin neon outlines, particle soup, the same glow on every effect, perspective other than top-down three-quarter, text inside the effects.

**Format:** one sheet per boss, about 2048 × 2048 (or larger), a grid of rows, one effect per row (three panels each). Two sheets per boss is fine if the rows don't fit.

## Sheet 1: Grubnak, the Glutton Chef (a burning kitchen)

Palette: cooking-fire orange and yellow, grease brown, charred black, a little red sauce.

1. **Chop Chop / Tenderize**: the cleaver coming down (Tenderize is his tankbuster). The raised cleaver, the chop splitting the floor with sparks, and the gouge left behind.
2. **Rolling Meatballs**: a huge meatball rolling across the kitchen. The lane in front of it, the hit where it meets a wall, and the gravy trail it leaves.
3. **Gut Blast**: a blast you hide from behind a prop. A wall of hot breath rolling out from him, with the shadow of cover cut out of it.
4. **Grease Fire / Flare-Up**: a grease stain catching light. The puddle (the warning), the whoosh of flame, then a guttering smear that burns off; and the whole stove line going up at once.
5. **Flambé**: a long stripe of burning liquor across the room.
6. **Belly Flop**: him landing belly-first; a shockwave ring with flagstones and pans jumping.
7. **Order Up! / Service!**: the order rail's plates (towers you stand in, in order). A plate arriving in a column of steam, and how *Stuffed* looks on a hero after.
8. **Boil Over / Simmer**: the stockpot boiling over in waves.
9. **Gorge** (the soft enrage): him swallowing the room, a vortex of food and air pulling in.

## Sheet 2: Queen Skitterra, the Spider Broodmother (a web cave)

Palette: venom acid green, silk off-white, a pink-purple for her bite, dark cave blue.

1. **Venom Mist** (raidwide): green fog rolling in from the walls.
2. **Venom Spit**: a lobbed spit at one player; the bead trail locking on, the splash, and the slowing pool it leaves.
3. **Eightfold Lunge**: a raised, segmented leg striking a cone; the two opposite cones and the turning safe side.
4. **Silk Wrap** (tankbuster): silk strands wheeling in and snapping round the tank, who's held fast in thread for a moment.
5. **Fang Rush / Silk Reel** (Hard / both): her charging along a line, dragging silk; a thread shot at a player and reeled taut.
6. **Acid Deluge**: acid rain over the whole cave, with the dry shadow of each pillar.
7. **Spiderling hatch**: an egg clutch splitting and a spiderling crawling out; and an enraged spiderling (red, swollen).
8. **From the Dark / Ceiling Ambush**: her shadow running along a thread, and her dropping onto the floor in a burst of silk and dust.
9. **The Collapse**: a ring of floor shuddering, then falling away into the dark, leaving only the silk spokes.

## Sheet 3: Volkar, the Storm Titan (a mountain summit in a storm)

Palette: electric cyan and white, storm-cloud slate, gold for + charge, deep blue for − charge.

1. **Thunderclap** (raidwide): the summit darkening under rolling clouds, then the clap.
2. **Titan Fist** (tankbuster): lightning gathering along a cone, then a fist-shaped bolt.
3. **Thunder Crash**: a storm cloud over a player, and the bolt that splits between the pair standing under it.
4. **Lightning Rods**: an iron rod driven into the rock, catching a bolt; the rod left glowing.
5. **Gale Slam**: him drawing breath (air pouring in), then the wind throwing everyone back; the still air behind a rod.
6. **Storm Walls**: two walls of wind turning slowly round him.
7. **The Squall Line**: the safe circle drifting round him, with the electrified storm outside it. How should the live edge look?
8. **Heavens Fall**: a column of sky coming down on one spot that the whole party holds up together.
9. **Chain Lightning / Ball Lightning**: a bolt leaping player to player; crackling orbs drifting across the summit.
10. **Polarity**: a + player and a − player paired (a calm bond), and two of the same charge arcing at each other.

## Sheet 4: The Hollow King, Lord of the Barrow (a burial hall)

Palette: grave-light green, Doom violet, old gold, cold moonlight blue.

1. **Royal Gaze**: an eye opening over him; what being caught looks like (frozen, Dread on you).
2. **Barrow Knell** (raidwide): the toll rolling out in rings.
3. **Grave Cleave** (tankbuster): a cone of grave-light, and the bleed after.
4. **Sceptre Sweep / Barrow Stomp / Crown of Graves**: his three swings. A wide cone; a circle round him with cracks; everywhere but close to him, with hands reaching up from the floor. These three must be told apart in a second.
5. **The Echo Knights**: a ghost knight remembering a swing (a flash) and replaying it, a paler version of his.
6. **Royal Decree**: a wave of grave-light rolling down the hall, with the shadows of lids and rubble cut out of it.
7. **Royal Doom**: the violet skull sigil over a head; the lantern's light curing it; the Long Night's single lantern being carried.
8. **Judgement**: the banners blazing grave-green, and three swings landing in quick order.
9. **Court of Bones**: a coffin glowing as it wakes, bursting open, a skeleton climbing out.
10. **Funeral March** (Hard): ranks of ghost soldiers with levelled spears tramping across the hall.

## Also useful (optional, one small sheet)

- **Fury** (the boss hitting harder as a slow party falls behind) and **an enrage cast**: what "he's about to end it" looks like.
- **Telegraph shapes on their own**: one circle, one cone, one line, one donut, one stack and one spread marker in your style, without any boss theme. Every boss's floor warnings are built from these.
