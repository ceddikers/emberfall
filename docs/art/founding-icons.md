# The Founding: icon briefs (items, skills, boons, builder's board, quests, HUD)

The Founding is the story of how Emberfall was founded: wardens and settlers in a valley of ash beasts, before there was a town. Everything here is frontier founders' gear, not the raid's royal, boss-themed kit: rough timber, hammered iron, leather and rawhide, lamp oil, ember and ash, moon-silver, river water and river stones, bluebells. Nothing is polished, jewelled or ornate; it is made by people with a few tools and one hard winter.

Same cartoon/anime fantasy look as `item-icons.md` and `ability-icons.md`, so the Founding's bag, hotbar and board match the rest of the game. The numbered one-paragraph format is the same too: the number is how I match each painting to its key.

## Sheet order

Please keep the numbered order below in an even grid, **5 per row**. Several sheets are fine; the numbering **continues across sections** (it does not restart), so a sheet is just the next run of numbers, and the number tells me which key a painting belongs to. Each entry ends with its file key in brackets; all files go in `client/founding/icons/` as `<key>.png`, square (256 for items, skills and the board, 128 is plenty for boons, quests and HUD).

## Styles (paste the right one before each section's icons)

### Items (section 1, and the board's squad upgrades)

> Fantasy cartoon game item icon in a clean cel-shaded anime style, like a prop sheet for a hand-drawn fantasy animated series. One object, centred, filling about 75% of the square, with a chunky, clear silhouette that reads at 48 pixels. Confident line art: a thick dark outline around the object, thinner lines inside. Flat colours with one hard-edged shadow tone and a small hard white highlight. Magic and gems are flat, bright shapes of solid colour, never glow. A plain, flat, dark background with a slightly lighter circle behind the object. Light from the upper left.

**Avoid (negative prompt):** glow, neon, bloom, lens flare, sparkles, particles, soft gradients, airbrushing, realistic rendering, photo textures, chrome shine, hands, people, busy fine detail, frame, border, text, watermark. For the Founding also avoid: crowns, royal gold-and-jewel styling, polished showroom armour. Materials look hand-made and a little worn.

**Layout:** weapons are diagonal (handle bottom-left, tip top-right); pouches, lamps, bottles and cups stand upright; armour is shown on its own, front view. Lamplight and fire are flat shapes of solid colour.

**Rarity** (Common grey, Uncommon green, Rare blue, Legendary purple, Mythic orange in the Founding's frame colours) is drawn by the game as the icon's frame, so the paintings themselves don't need it. Commons are plain and worn, Uncommons add one small trick, Rares are properly made, Legendaries are the pride of a smith, and Mythics (the last eight) can be a touch grander: richer materials and one bold flat accent colour.

**Hero items** (marked "Bastion item", "Cleric item" and so on) carry a small accent of their hero's colour: Bastion slate-blue, Cleric ochre-gold, Pyromancer burnt orange, Ranger moss green.

### Skills (section 2)

> Fantasy cartoon game ability icon in a clean cel-shaded anime style, like props drawn for a hand-drawn fantasy animated series. One bold object, filling about 70% of the square, with a chunky, clear silhouette that reads at 40 pixels. Confident line art: a thick dark outline around the object, thinner lines inside. Flat colours with one hard-edged shadow tone and a small hard white highlight. A limited palette: the background colour, one or two object colours, and one bright accent used only where the action happens. One light from the upper left. Motion is shown with a few clean, tapered speed lines, and magic as flat cartoon shapes in solid colour (flames with curled tips, bold rings, star bursts), never as glow. A plain flat background in the hero's colour, with a simple darker vignette shape or a few bold diagonal bands.

**Avoid (negative prompt):** glow, neon, bloom, lens flare, sparkles, particles, soft gradients, airbrushing, realistic rendering, chrome shine, hands, people, floating wizards, busy fine detail, frame, border, text, watermark.

**Rules:** keep the subject inside the central circle (some slots are drawn round). No people: objects and signs only. Different silhouettes within a hero's row (diagonal, round, upright, spread). One bright spot per icon, where the hit or the magic is. Do one hero at a time as a row of five (Q, W, E, R, then the passive), so each hero shares one background and palette. The passive is drawn like the others, but as a small badge-like sign rather than an action.

### Boons (section 3)

> Fantasy cartoon game boon emblem in a clean cel-shaded anime style. One bold symbol, centred inside a round medallion, filling about 65% of the circle, with a chunky silhouette that still reads at 24 pixels. Thick dark outline, flat colours with one hard shadow tone and a small hard white highlight. The medallion is a flat disc in the quest's colour with a slightly darker rim ring; the corners outside the disc stay plain dark. Light from the upper left. Magic and fire are flat shapes of solid colour, never glow.

**Avoid (negative prompt):** glow, neon, bloom, sparkles, particles, soft gradients, realistic rendering, hands, people, busy fine detail, frame, text, numbers, watermark.

**Colour rule:** the three boons of one quest share a disc colour, so a boon reads as belonging to its quest. Each group's disc colour is given in its heading. Each boon is a different symbol in that colour family with one accent.

### Builder's board (section 4)

Towers use the **Skills** style, with a stone-and-timber background in a dusty tan, one tower per icon, seen from the front and slightly above. The three squad upgrades use the **Items** style with a plain dark disc.

### Quests (section 5)

Thick dark outline, flat colours, one hard shadow tone, one small white highlight. A plain flat background in the quest's colour (the same colours as its boons). Light from the upper left. No glow.

Avoid the same as the boons.
