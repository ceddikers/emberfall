# Boss VFX sheets: copy-paste prompts

Six prompts, two sheets per boss, the same layout as the Grubnak pair. Each block stands alone: paste one block into your image tool and you get one sheet. (Grubnak is done.)

Two things changed after the Grubnak sheets:
- **Each move's warning panel should be unique to that move.** On the Grubnak sheets most warnings were plain orange tiles; only Gut Blast's (the cone with the crate's shadow) told you which move it was.
- **The boss is described** so the figure in the panels matches the game.

## Queen Skitterra, the Spider Broodmother: part 1 of 2

```
A game VFX concept sheet titled "BOSS QUEEN SKITTERRA THE SPIDER BROODMOTHER (Part 1 of 2)", in a clean, hand-painted, stylised fantasy RPG style. Top-down three-quarter view, about 50 degrees from overhead. Five rows, one effect per row, each row in three panels left to right: (1) THE WARNING: the floor marking for this move, shaped and decorated so it could only be this move, plus the boss's tell (what her body does while she casts); (2) THE IMPACT: the bold main shape, a hot white core, a saturated mid colour and dark inky outer edges; (3) THE AFTERMATH: what stays on the floor for a few seconds. A small grey hero silhouette in each panel for scale. Flat, readable shapes with hard edges; soft glow only at the very core. Plain dark slate-grey background. A short handwritten label above each row; no other text.

The boss: a huge spider queen, body dark plum-purple with crimson markings and gold accents, eight long jointed legs, glowing eyes, fangs dripping green venom. She lives in a web cave of rock pillars strung with silk.

Palette: venom acid green, silk off-white, pink-purple for her bites, dark cave blue.

1. Venom Mist (hits the whole room): green fog rolling in from the cave walls, her fangs dripping during the cast; the aftermath, a thin green haze settling.
2. Venom Spit: a lobbed glob at one player. Warning: a line of venom beads locking onto them. Impact: an acid splash. Aftermath: a bubbling green pool that slows.
3. Eightfold Lunge: a raised, segmented leg with a red tip striking a cone, and the opposite leg striking the opposite cone. Show the turning safe side.
4. Silk Wrap (tankbuster): silk strands wheeling in and snapping tight around the tank, who is held fast in thread for a moment.
5. Fang Rush / Silk Reel: her charging along a line, dragging a trail of silk behind her; a single thread shot at a player and pulled taut.
```

## Volkar, the Storm Titan: part 1 of 2

```
A game VFX concept sheet titled "BOSS VOLKAR THE STORM TITAN (Part 1 of 2)", in a clean, hand-painted, stylised fantasy RPG style. Top-down three-quarter view, about 50 degrees from overhead. Six rows, one effect per row, each row in three panels left to right: (1) THE WARNING: the floor marking for this move, shaped and decorated so it could only be this move, plus the boss's tell (what his body does while he casts); (2) THE IMPACT: the bold main shape, a hot white core, a saturated mid colour and dark inky outer edges; (3) THE AFTERMATH: what stays on the floor for a few seconds. A small grey hero silhouette in each panel for scale. Flat, readable shapes with hard edges; soft glow only at the very core. Plain dark slate-grey background. A short handwritten label above each row; no other text.

The boss: a giant titan made of slate-blue storm stone, cracks glowing electric cyan, huge stone fists, standing still in the middle of a round mountain summit above the clouds. Four standing stones stand round him.

Palette: electric cyan and white lightning, storm-cloud slate, gold for the + charge, deep blue for the − charge.

1. Thunderclap (hits everyone): the summit darkening under rolling clouds, then the clap of lightning.
2. Titan Fist (tankbuster): lightning gathering along a cone in front of him, then a stone fist wrapped in lightning slamming down.
3. Thunder Crash: a small storm cloud over a player, and the bolt that splits between the two players standing under it together.
4. Lightning Rods: an iron rod driven into the rock, catching a bolt from the sky; the rod left standing, glowing and crackling.
5. Gale Slam: him drawing a huge breath (air pouring in toward him), then the wind blasting everyone outward; the still, sheltered air behind a rod.
6. Storm Walls: two walls of wind and lightning turning slowly around him like the hands of a clock.
```

## The Hollow King, Lord of the Barrow: part 1 of 2

```
A game VFX concept sheet titled "BOSS THE HOLLOW KING, LORD OF THE BARROW (Part 1 of 2)", in a clean, hand-painted, stylised fantasy RPG style. Top-down three-quarter view, about 50 degrees from overhead. Six rows, one effect per row, each row in three panels left to right: (1) THE WARNING: the floor marking for this move, shaped and decorated so it could only be this move, plus the boss's tell (what his body does while he casts); (2) THE IMPACT: the bold main shape, a hot white core, a saturated mid colour and dark inky outer edges; (3) THE AFTERMATH: what stays on the floor for a few seconds. A small grey hero silhouette in each panel for scale. Flat, readable shapes with hard edges; soft glow only at the very core. Plain dark slate-grey background. A short handwritten label above each row; no other text.

The boss: a dead king in dark iron armour, a tattered cape, a tarnished gold crown, ghostly green light where his face should be, a greatsword and a sceptre. He holds court in a long burial hall of flagstones, coffins along the walls and green-flamed braziers.

Palette: grave-light green, Doom violet, old gold, cold moonlight blue.

1. His three swings, side by side, which must be told apart in a second: Sceptre Sweep (a wide cone in front of him), Barrow Stomp (a circle round him with cracks spreading through the flagstones), Crown of Graves (everywhere except close to him, with skeletal hands reaching up out of the floor).
2. Royal Gaze: a great eye opening above him; what being caught looks like (the hero frozen, grey stone creeping over them).
3. Barrow Knell (hits everyone): the toll of a bell rolling out across the hall in rings.
4. Grave Cleave (tankbuster): a cone of grave-light, the greatsword coming down, and the bleeding wound after.
5. The Echo Knights: a ghost knight by the wall remembering one of his swings (a flash of it on the knight), then replaying it as a paler version from where it stands.
6. Coffin Lids and the Royal Decree: a stone coffin lid skidding across the hall and stopping; then a wave of green grave-light rolling down the hall from the throne, with the lid's shadow left safe behind it.
```
