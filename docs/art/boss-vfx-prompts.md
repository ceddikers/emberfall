# Boss VFX sheets: copy-paste prompts

Six prompts, two sheets per boss, the same layout as the Grubnak pair. Each block stands alone: paste one block into your image tool and you get one sheet. (Grubnak is done.)

Two things changed after the Grubnak sheets:
- **Each move's warning panel should be unique to that move.** On the Grubnak sheets most warnings were plain orange tiles; only Gut Blast's (the cone with the crate's shadow) told you which move it was.
- **The boss is described** so the figure in the panels matches the game.

## Queen Skitterra, the Spider Broodmother: part 1 of 2

```
A game VFX concept sheet titled "BOSS QUEEN SKITTERRA THE SPIDER BROODMOTHER (Part 1 of 2)", in a clean, hand-painted, stylised fantasy RPG style. Top-down three-quarter view, about 50 degrees from overhead. Five rows, one effect per row, each row in three panels left to right: (1) THE WARNING: the floor marking for this move, shaped and decorated so it could only be this move, plus the boss's tell (what her body does while she casts); (2) THE IMPACT: the bold main shape, a hot white core, a saturated mid colour and dark inky outer edges; (3) THE AFTERMATH: what stays on the floor for a few seconds. A small grey hero silhouette in each panel for scale. Flat, readable shapes with hard edges; soft glow only at the very core. Plain dark slate-grey background. A short handwritten label above each row; no other text.

The boss: a huge spider queen, body dark plum-purple with crimson markings and gold accents, eight long jointed legs, glowing eyes, fangs dripping green venom. She lives in a web cave of rock pillars strung with silk.

Palette: venom acid green, silk off-white, pink-purple for her bites, dark cave blue.

1. Venom Mist (hits the whole room): green fog rolling in from the cave walls, her fangs dripping during the cast; the aftermath, a thin green haze settling.
2. Venom Spit: a lobbed glob at one player. Warning: a line of venom beads locking onto them. Impact: an acid splash. Aftermath: a bubbling green pool that slows.
3. Eightfold Lunge: a raised, segmented leg with a red tip striking a cone, and the opposite leg striking the opposite cone. Show the turning safe side.
4. Silk Wrap (tankbuster): silk strands wheeling in and snapping tight around the tank, who is held fast in thread for a moment.
5. Fang Rush / Silk Reel: her charging along a line, dragging a trail of silk behind her; a single thread shot at a player and pulled taut.
```

```

```
