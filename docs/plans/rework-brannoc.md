# Brannoc rework: pairs, the stones, and a Last Round with three jobs

*2026-10-25. Review: fun 7 on both; "where will it bounce?" is a clear, learnable idea and the titan on the ring of stone is a strong beat. Normal fair; Hard's layers (Twin Wheels, Riposte, Double Grip) are painted. Problems: Deep Call shoved everyone to the rim just before Breaker's wave bounced back off it; the Sand Furrow zone cost bots 76 hits on Normal; the Hard enrage was too tight (clears up to 596 s against a cast at 592 s); the titan acts were single moves with long quiet gaps for the tank and healer. Goal: fun 8 or more, without taking anything demanding out of Hard.*

## His answers to "Finding a fight's idea" (docs/DESIGN.md)

1. **What is happening in this room?** A champion still fighting for a crowd of ghosts in a sunken arena; halfway through, the star claims him and he rises from the sea as the titan of a broken ring of stone.
2. **What question does the fight keep asking?** "Where will it bounce?" Now also "what will it bounce off?": the wall, and in the sea the stones on the ring.
3. **What is the rule a player learns?** Everything he throws is a path that bounces, and the whole path is drawn before it moves. A new move is the same rule on a new thing (a wheel, a giant wheel, a wave), and later two moves are drawn on the floor at once.
4. **How does the fight warn?** He turns to the first wall and holds that facing; every bounce is drawn, with a blue ring where it rests. A Deep Call is cast and shown as swells closing on the middle. Nothing is hidden on any difficulty.
5. **Acts.** First Blood, Crowd-Pleaser, The Tide Rises (the seam), The Tidewrought, Last Round.
6. **Ending.** Three jobs at once: a Grip held on someone (break the hand), a six-bounce wheel (catch the rings) and the fist on the tank (walk it clear). Every earlier lesson at the same moment, then The Final Bell.
7. **What does Hard change?** Twin Wheels (also from the titan), Double Grip, Riposte, Riptide, faster casts.
8. **Luck.** Who the Leap lands on, who the hand takes, the wheel's angle: random, drawn in full ahead, so it is reading and not luck. The Leap's landing now decides where the next wheel is thrown from, so the party's choice shapes the road.
9. **Tank and healer.** The tank takes Hammer Heel and, in the sea, the titan's strikes and the fist, and walks the fist clear of a party the sea has just dragged together. The healer has the raidwides, the catches and a bleeding hero.
10. **Its own.** A boss who becomes the summon, with a new bar, a new room and new music; and a rule (bounces) that includes the room's own props.
11. **What will players talk about?** The Last Round's three jobs, and the stone that turned a giant wheel back into the middle.

## Changes

1. **Deep Call drags everyone in** (to within a step or two of the middle, never closer) instead of shoving them to the rim. No more standing against the wall as a wave comes back off it. It is now a set-up: the sea drags the party together and the next move lands on the gathering. Tell and impact redrawn (swells and arrows closing on the middle, rings collapsing).
2. **Pairs.** Pull then **Fist Slam** (called as the pull lands); pull then **Sunwheel Unbound**; **Breaker** then an Unbound drawn while the wave still runs; **Grip** then Breaker (the held hero is in the wave's lane); **Victory Leap** then a **Sunwheel** thrown from where he landed (Acts I and II).
3. **The stones bounce wheels.** In the sea the four debris are posts for Sunwheel Unbound and for Twin Wheels from the titan (the wheel's centre turns at the stone's radius plus its own). Catch rings are always placed on open floor, clear of the stones and of every other stretch of the road (the planner tries many throws and keeps the one with the most room).
4. **Tank and healer work.** More tank hits in the sea come from the fist paired with the pull (Deep Call, then Fist Slam) and from Grip then Breaker; the titan's own strikes were tried and dropped (a runeguard tank could not live through them with the weaker healer).
5. **Last Round** is paired throughout and ends on three jobs at once (Grip, six-bounce wheel, fist on the tank). Hard adds Twin Wheels from the titan before it, and once in the middle of Act III. Last Round's calms are 10 to 11 s.
6. **Sand Furrow** zone lasts 12 s, not 16 s.
7. **Enrage and pacing.** Hard's calms between sets are a sixteenth shorter (the same moves, sooner: more pressure, and a script that ends at 9:11), and The Final Bell casts at 612 s (lands 10:20, about a minute after the script ends). Normal unchanged (10:15, script ends 9:05). Normal's calms are the original ones; shorter ones starved the weaker line-up (grovekeeper healer, runeguard tank) of healing time between the raidwides.
8. **Journal.** Deep Call rewritten for all 23 heroes; Sunwheel Unbound mentions the stones for all 23. Normal's Grip hand has more health (30000, was 24000) so breaking it takes real focus. No new cast names, so no new sounds, icons or voice lines.

## Kept

Telegraphs never hidden, 8 to 10 minutes, one enrage, the closing passes for a slow party, Hard's four Normal-never moves (Twin Wheels, Double Grip, Riposte, Riptide).

## Third pass (2026-10-26): cool factor

Beat on top of the existing fight, no new cast names (so no new sounds or icons). **Look and feel** (`brannoc-fx.js`, all cosmetic): the wheel winds up in his hand before it is thrown and leaves it with a burst; a bounce off a stone rings differently from one off the rim (white-blue star on the painted road, chips of rock, a ring, the camera holds for the giant wheel); the ghost crowd gasps at each bounce and roars at a catch; the camera holds when a giant wheel goes through a hero, when Deep Call drags the room in (a streak behind every hero), when the hand is broken and when he stands up out of the sea; the Last Round opens with rings of pale light through the sand. **The whiff:** a hero a wheel, wave, furrow, banner or leap did not hit, who stood within a body of it (or dashed through a wheel's lane unhurt), sees a sliver of the wheel's rim (or the wave's lash) go by, with sparks, a ring and the crowd's gasp; your own hero gets a flash at the screen edge, a flinch of the camera and a soft sound. It is only drawn for a hero the blow did not hit and was close to, so it rewards a dodge and never marks safe floor. **Mechanics:** Hard's Twin Wheels second wheel is thrown 1.1 s after the first (the crossing is clear for a moment between them: a hero who reads both roads can cut through, one who lingers is caught by the second; the foot of each road wears a badge of one or two dots). Bots: Normal `GEAR=post3` 83 of 90 (as before), the weaker line-up still clears about 65%; Hard `GEAR=post4` 56 of 90 (62%, was 50 to 56).

## Fourth pass (2026-10-26): fun pass, tank and healer beats, Hard shuffles

*Found:* the longest tank stretch (Normal / Hard worst case over many pulls) was Act I 41 / 79 s, Act II 57 / 55 s (only three Heels in 141 s), Act III 47 / 79 s, Last Round 33 / 42 s; Acts I and II were ~270 s of wheel, leap, heel, roar; Hard shuffled only Acts I and III; Deep Call pulled each hero in by at most 170 and never closer than 70 from the middle, so a party on opposite sides stayed apart; Breaker's wave road was painted only ~1.6 s before it landed.

*Changed (existing moves only, no new cast names):* **Pairs.** Roar of the Stands then Hammer Heel (the raidwide, then the stamps while the healer is still topping up) in Acts I and II; Act II also a Sunwheel (Twin Wheels on Hard) then a Heel once the road, and on Hard the Riposte, are done; Act III Sunwheel Unbound then Fist Slam while the giant wheel is still running (replaces a lone Fist Slam and a lone Unbound). Act I now has four Heels (was three), Act II five (was three), spaced 25 to 38 s apart; Act III's lone Fist moved to the head of the tail and Last Round's order puts the Grip then Breaker last before the three jobs. **Hard shuffles** now cover Acts II and the Last Round as well as I and III, through `mix()`, which rejects orders that leave the tank more than ~34 to 40 s without a Heel or fist, put two beats under 18 s apart, or set a Twin Wheels set next to another wheel set (two roads to re-read at once). **Deep Call** now really gathers: every hero beyond 130 of the middle is pulled in to 130 (heroes inside stay put), so the following Fist Slam or wheel lands on a party that is together. **Breaker**'s fist locks 1.8 s before it lands (was 1.0) and the wave road is drawn at that moment, so the road shows for 2.4 s or more before the first piece lands (was ~1.6 s); the origin cannot be known before the tank's fist stops following. After a throw in the sea the titan faces north again (the wheel's facing lock released and let him wander). Riposte left as is: the catcher is standing at the start of the kicked road and has ~2.2 s to step off a 120-wide lane, which is the point of the layer; its cast bar never replaces a real cast (it is only drawn while the boss is idle between sets).

### Review fixes (2026-10-26)

`mix()` no longer falls back to a "best of a bad lot": it tries every order of an act's middle sets once (Heap's algorithm, at most 40320) and draws one of the valid ones. Valid means the tank is never more than the act's limit (Act I 38 s, II 40, III 34, Last Round 40) without a Heel or fist, never two beats under 18 s apart, no two raidwides (Roar, Deep Call) landing under 18 s apart, and a Twin Wheels set never directly after another Twin Wheels or a Sunwheel Unbound set (relaxed from "never next to"). Valid orders on Hard: Act I 16 of 720, Act II 72 of 5040, Act III 226 of 40320, Last Round 5 of 24 (the Last Round is a tight arc; a Twin Wheels then fist pair was tried and dropped: it left no more valid orders and put a fist beside every breaker). Normal's written order is held to the same rules (Last Round: the second Deep Call now waits 14 s, so it lands 18 s after the first, and goes before the Breaker sets). The Heel after Act II's Twin Wheels waits for a Riposte still on the floor (no overlapping cast bars). Undertow Grip's crush on Hard is 22200 (x1.34 = 29750, Normal 27000; was 19000, lighter than Normal). Roar then Heel at 5.4 s was bot-checked with first-death logs: on Hard `post4` the first deaths were Undertow 12 of 31, early catches (tower) 11, Sand Furrow 4 and raidwide 3, none a Heel after a Roar, so it stays. Bots: Normal `GEAR=post3` 85 of 90 (94%), Hard `GEAR=post4` 59 of 90 (66%) on the usual seeds, 98 of 150 (65%) with two more seed sets; weak line-up Normal 16 of 30 (15 before this pass), Hard 4 of 30 (1 before).

*Final numbers (2026-10-26):* tuned with the existing multiplier, not structure. Roar 14000 to 14300 on Normal and 11200 to 11800 on Hard (Hard x1.34 is now x1.106 Normal, was x1.07); Deep Call 16400 to 17400 on Normal and 14000 to 14400 on Hard (x1.109); the Normal hand 30000 to 31000 health and Hard's 17000 to 16000 (Hard's hand stays the weaker one on purpose: Double Grip). ## Fifth pass (2026-10-26): Rebound, a move of the tank's own

*Found:* the tank's longest wait between beats of their own (Heel, Fist Slam, Breaker; worst over many pulls) was Normal 31 / 33 / 29 / 30 s (Acts I, II, III, Last Round) and Hard 34 / 35 / 34 / 38 s, and in the sea (no boss autos) the tank was truly idle between them. The busters cannot come more often (a Heel pair is about a whole tank).

*The move:* **Rebound**, his own rule ("where will it bounce?") aimed at the tank. He flings a small, fast wheel at them. A blue ring follows the tank about from the cast and lets go where they stand (the last 3.4 s of the hold on Normal, 3.2 s on Hard); from that moment the whole road is drawn, from him, off the wall (once on Normal, twice on Hard) and on to the ring. So the tank chooses the road by where they stand when the ring lets go (a spot where the road misses the party, a step out of a gathered party after Deep Call), the party reads it as they read every other throw, and the tank stands in the ring and catches it. Nobody in the ring and it bursts on the room (the Sunwheel's rule); on Hard the caught wheel is kicked back along a new road, the existing Riposte. The road is drawn at the lock with 1.5 s or more before the wheel moves and its first red piece lands (the wheel's speed is set so it reaches the ring as the ring resolves); nothing under 1.2 s, no painted safe spot. Reuses the cast anim 'throw', the brpath road, the brCatch ring and the wheel pieces, so no new art. Numbers: the catch 14000 on Normal, 11600 on Hard (x1.34 = 15544, x1.11), the lane 13000 / 11000; a Heel stamp is 17000 / 22000, a Sunwheel catch 18000.

*The road* (`aimPlan`): every thrown angle is tried and the ones whose last line runs through the ring are refined; of those the road with room round the ring (at least 140, then 105; the start at his feet does not count, so a tank in his face is fine) that does not run straight back along itself and is thrown nearest to straight at the tank is drawn. One bounce first (Hard two, then three, then one), then more; a tank who stands almost exactly on the line from him through the middle (or behind a stone in the sea) has no bounce road and the wheel comes straight (about one throw in ten from a random spot, `f.brannocAim` has the counts).

*Where it sits* (every act, replacing lone Sunwheels and Leaps so the script does not grow much): Act I Leap then Rebound (was Leap then Sunwheel), a lone Rebound where the lone Leap was, and one as the tail's first set (was a Sunwheel); Act II the second wheel of the double Sunwheel, the Furrow's partner (was a Leap) and the Leap's partner (was a Sunwheel); Act III a lone Rebound (was a lone Unbound) and Deep Call then Rebound (was Deep Call then Unbound; the tank carries the ring out of the gathered party); Last Round Deep Call then Rebound (was a wheel), and its second set waits 11 s. `mix()` knows the light beat: a Rebound may follow another beat 10 s later (13 s after a Heel or fist) and a heavy beat may follow it 10 s later, instead of 18; limits tightened to 30 / 28 / 28 / 28 s (valid Hard orders: 14 of 720, 36 of 5040, 44 of 40320, 4 of 24).

*Numbers.* Tank's longest wait per act, Normal before / after: 31 / 29, 33 / 26, 29 / 24, 30 / 25 (Act I and II's top is the first beat after the pull; after it no gap is over 20); Hard, worst of 80 pulls, before / after: 34 / 29, 35 / 23, 34 / 25, 38 / 27. Script ends 9:18 Normal and 9:26 Hard (was 9:12 / 9:20); The Final Bell unchanged.
