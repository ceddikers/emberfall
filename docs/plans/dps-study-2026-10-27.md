# DPS study, 2026-10-27

Every DPS spec, no talents, on all 12 bosses at each set's tuning gear (Normal: fresh / post1 / post3; Hard: mid / post2 / post4). 48 parties x 2 pulls per boss and mode, tanks and healers varied (comp-sweep seed 5000), about 12 pulls per spec per boss. **Perfect** = normal bots; **human** = human-like DPS (bots.js BotHuman, calibrated to a real Skitterra Hard pull), tank and healer stay bots. Measured after the 2026-10-27 bot fixes and class buffs (uncommitted).

## Follow-up, same day

- **DPS lifts (no nerfs):** Lone Wolf +18%, Phantom +14%, Comet +12%, Understudy +6%, Dancer +5% (specs.js potMult plus a matching spec `balance`). Human-like index afterwards: lowest spec 94 on both difficulties (was 82), the lifted five 94-101.
- **Lanternkeeper:** Beacon's glow cut 8% -> 12%, and a Wayfarer's small lights carry the same cut. Controlled line-up: Beacon 10 -> 17 clears of 48, Wayfarer 1 -> 9 (Cleric and Tidecaller about 30).
- **Why human parties wiped on Hard:** almost never the enrage. A single avoidable hit took 1300-1800 off about 1100 HP, the fight went on a body short, and the next raidwides finished it.
- **Retune for a human party** (CLAUDE.md targets): per-boss `hardDmg` cut about 10-20% (Skitterra, Volkar also lose a little Hard health), and a new per-boss `normalDmg` (fight.js, raid-host's bot view) for the Normal fights that were out of line: Vhalgrath, Azerath, Corvane (health), Morrow-Oak, Caelum.
- **Results, base line-up with `HUMAN=dps`, 48 pulls per boss:** Hard 18-35% (finales: Hollow King 18, Azerath 25, Caelum 20); Normal set 1 75-96%, Azerath 73, Morrow-Oak 77, Corvane 70, Vhalgrath 72, Caelum 60. Perfect bots: Hard 58-100%.
