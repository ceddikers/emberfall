# The Ultimate: build notes (the contract)

The design is in `docs/plans/2026-10-ultimate-raid.md`; read it first. This page is the contract between the fight script (`client/js/arena/bosses/sovereign.js`, Wave 2) and everything the clients draw, play and say. The model is Caelum Vey: `docs/plans/2026-10-caelum-build.md`, `caelum.js`, `caelum-art.js` and `caelum-fx.js`. His Hard *Chorus of the Fallen* (`roomev 'clchorus:<n>'`, drawn in `caelum-fx.js`) is the nearest thing to an echo.

Each wave adds its rows here as it builds. A field nobody has built yet is marked *(planned)*.

- **Boss id:** `sovereign`, with the name "The Cinder Sovereign". It is one difficulty only, played as `normal` internally.
- **Tag and message prefix:** `sv`.
- **Rooms** (fight.js `setForm`, sent as `roomev` `k: 'form'`; `ArenaView.buildFloor` takes `sovereign` and `sovereign:<form>`):

## Set pieces (Wave 1, E1: engine, not Sovereign-specific)

The script calls `f.setPiece({ id, dur, invul = 3, run(f) })` as a step on its course.

- **For its first `invul` seconds** the boss takes no damage. Hits show *Immune* to the hitter, and damage over an HP gate is dropped.
- **For all of `dur`:**
  - The hard enrage, the course's own clock (its next steps and seams) and every timer running on the party are paused: debuffs, DoTs, Doom, burns and the Recovering window. They pick up where they left off.
  - Cooldowns, Ultimate charge and the dash keep running.
  - Nothing may hit the party.
  - Bots hold their Ultimates and the Limit Break.
- **The host sends** `{ t: 'setpiece', id, dur, invul }` at the start. Clients read it to play the set piece and to show the invulnerability.

| Set piece id | When | dur (start) |
|---|---|---|
| `svdescent` | The pull | 6 |
| `svburnback` | 78%, into Act II | 8 |
| `svrebirth` | 50% wall done, into Act III | 12 |
| `sveveryname` | 20%, into Act IV | 10 |
| `svlastflame` | The kill (no invul needed) | 14 |
| `svemberfall` | The enrage | 8 |

**Built (E1).** `fight.js` `FightSim.setPiece(o)`, tested by `tools/setpiece-test.js`.
- Call it from a course step: `[delay, () => f.setPiece({ id, dur, invul = 3, run(f) }), name]`. It returns false before the pull or with no `dur`. `run(f)` is called at once. A second set piece inside the first only adds the time that runs past it.
- **Clocks.** Each tick of the set piece pushes back every pending event (steps, follow-ups, the enrage), the course seams and end, the boss swing timer, and on every player the debuffs, Vulnerability, Damage Down, Raise Sickness and the Recovering window (`s.debuffs`, `vulnEnd`, `dmgDownEnd`, `weakEnd`, `recoverEnd`). Shields, mitigation, heals over time, cooldowns, Ultimate charge and dashes keep running. The shift is continuous, so the party frames and the upcoming-moves feed read as stopped.
- **A script's own timers** (Doom, a burn, anything kept in a closure) must register `f.onSetPiece(a => ...)`: it is called every tick of the set piece with the seconds `a` to push them back by. Nothing is registered by default.
- **Boss.** Hits are dropped while `t < sp.invulEnd` (onEventIn), so nothing crosses a gate. `f.sp = { id, start, end, invulEnd }` and `f.spShift` (total seconds stopped) are the state. A Time Stop is refused during one (`pauseBlock` says `set piece`).
- **Party.** `damagePlayer` returns 0 for everything during `t < sp.end` except kinds `self`, `ledger`, `settle` and `lb`. Telegraphs already on the floor still resolve, harmlessly, so start a set piece with a clear floor.
- **Bots.** `f.setPieceHold()` is true until `max(end, invulEnd)`. `raid-host.js` `botLimit` and the `BotAI.ult` press check it. `bots.js` is untouched.
- **Net.** `{ t: 'setpiece', id, dur, invul }` once at the start. `main.js` stores `raid.setpiece = { id, until, invulUntil }` (client `now` seconds). Hits on the boss show *Immune* (quiet text, no flinch) while `now < invulUntil`.
- **Enrage display.** The boss broadcast (`fb`) carries `pz`, the seconds stood still so far. `main.js` keeps `raid.fightPaused = msg.pz` and the enrage countdown and the moon use `fightTime - fightPaused`. The fight clock itself keeps running.
- **Not done.** The UI does not stop the player pressing T or the Limit Break into the invul window (only bots hold). The set piece is not in `tools/check.js` yet.

## The Sovereign's state (Wave 1, E2 draws it; Wave 2 sends it)

`roomev` `k: 'svhalo'` with `{ lit, call, wheel }`:
- `lit` is a 12-bit mask, where bit i is sigil i still lit.
- `call` is the sigil being called now, or -1.
- `wheel` is 1 once the halo has opened into the wheel of blades (Act III on).

The latest message is the state.

`st.props` the art reads (set in `main.js` from `raid`, the way Caelum's `sky` is):
- `svLit`, `svCall` and `svWheel`, from `svhalo`.
- `svBladeSide`: 1 for right, -1 for left, which is the Ashen Sweep's tell.
- `svForm`: `hall`, `forge`, `crater` or `square`. The body evolves with it, easing over about 2 s (see the design's "It evolves through the fight").
- `svGutter`: 0..1, the fire draining at the kill.

**Poses** (bcast name to anim): Kindling `kindling` · Brand `brand` · Ashen Sweep `sweep` · Cupped Ember `ember` · Scatter `scatter` · Halo Turn `haloturn` · the call `call` · Rebirth `rebirth` · The Sovereign's Dance `dance` · Crescent `crescent` · Lance `lance` · Pyre `pyre` · Emberfall `enrage`.

**Built (E2), `client/js/sovereign-art.js`:** `SV_ART.body` is registered as `BOSS_ART.sovereign`. It reads these `st.props` (all optional; absent means lit, no call, no wheel, right hand, hall):
- `svLit` (12-bit mask), `svCall` (sigil index or -1), `svWheel` (0 or 1, eased in), `svBladeSide` (1 or -1; the hand swap is a smooth turn).
- `svForm` (`hall`, `forge`, `crater`, `square`): the figure evolves, easing over about 2 s. Hall is held back (short blade, small halo, the hem smoulders). Forge adds slag plates, a longer hotter blade and thicker smoke. Crater burns the robe to a molten core, makes the eyes one white slit and puts star-glints in the smoke. Square is white-gold, in tatters, with the longest blade and a bare bright core. The wheel itself still comes from `svWheel`.
- `svGutter` (0..1, new, for the kill): the fire drains, the core gutters, the flames sink and drop motes.
- Poses are held props named `'sv' + Name`: `svKindling`, `svBrand`, `svSweep`, `svEmber`, `svScatter`, `svHaloturn`, `svCallPose` (not `svCall`, which is the sigil index), `svRebirth`, `svDance`, `svCrescent`, `svLance`, `svPyre`, `svEnrage`. `CAST_POSE` in `boss-vfx.js` should map each cast to one of these (call: `[['svCallPose', 0.5]]`). `svPose: '<name>'` does the same (used by the preview).
- A cast's swing is read from `st.action` (`{type: 'attack', t0, dur}`: wind-up to 30%, blow to 55%, back to 100%, as Caelum). Haloturn, call, dance, rebirth and enrage are held moves with no swing: they ramp in over 1, 0.8, 1, 3.4 and 1.2 s from the moment their prop first shows (so the prop must stay set for the length of the move).
- The halo is drawn in the world's frame (it does not turn with the heading) on a clock kept on `st` (`st.svSpin`). **`SV_ART.sigilPos(st, i)`** returns `{x, y, a, r}`, sigil i's offset from the boss in game px, for E3's flames to fly from. It needs the same `st` the boss is drawn with.
- Preview: `window.__svArtPreview(canvas, opts)` and `tools/preview/sovereign-art.html` (serve the repo root and open it; `?cell=N&bare&z=2` shows one cell large, `&t=` starts at a time, `&freeze` holds it). Not wired yet: `main.js` setting the props, `CAST_POSE`, `st.action` for the boss's casts.

## Echoes (Wave 1, E3 draws them; Waves 2–5 send them)

`roomev` `k: 'svecho:<n>'`, one per echo, n counting up through the pull:

| Field | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `who` | Boss id, from the echo order above |
| `sig` | Its sigil index; the flame flies from there on the halo |
| `x, y` | Where it rises, and where its floor sigil burns in |
| `fly` | Seconds for the flame's flight; 0 in Act IV, where it rises from its floor sigil |
| `rise` | Seconds to assemble from cinders |
| `dur` | Total seconds on the floor, crumble included; -1 means it stays until told |
| `face` | The angle it faces |
| `kneel` | 1 if it kneels to the Sovereign first. Every echo kneels except the Hollow King. |

- **Ending an echo:** `roomev` `k: 'svecho:<n>'` with `{ end: 1 }` crumbles it now.
- **A pose:** `roomev` `k: 'svechoact:<n>'` with `{ anim, dur }` makes it play a pose (Corvane's stance, the Hollow King's bell).
- **Lying down:** `{ anim: 'liedown' }` lies it down for the kill.

**Built (E3), `client/js/ult-echo.js`** (`ULT_ECHO`; preview `tools/preview/ult-echo.html`, or `window.__echoPreview(canvas, who, t, opts)`):
- **Ending an echo:** `roomEvent` replaces the whole `svecho:<n>` event and writes `end` itself (`start + dur`), so `{ end: 1 }` cannot be read as a flag. The art reads a second `svecho:<n>` message **with no `who`** as the end, crumbling at the time it arrived (and `ended: 1` if the message does carry a body). Send the end as `{ t: 'roomev', k: 'svecho:<n>' }` plus anything except `who`.
- **`dur`** counts from the call (the flame's flight included), crumble included; the crumble itself takes 2.6 s and the loose flakes linger about 2 s more, then the floor sigil fades over 3.5 s. `dur: -1` stays until ended.
- **Poses** (`svechoact:<n>` `{ anim, dur }`): `liedown` (any echo; it sinks and its cracks go dim, and it stays), Corvane `crane` / `comet` / `parting`, the Hollow King `bell` (or `knell`), `attack` / `strike` (the art's swing), and any other name is passed to the art as a prop (`props[anim] = 1`: `raise`, `roar`, `howl`, `maul`...). The kneel is drawn by the art: Corvane's own kneel, and a bow toward the Sovereign for the rest.
- **Hooks the art can replace:** `ULT_ECHO.hooks.boss()` (default: the live boss, else the room's middle) and `ULT_ECHO.hooks.halo(i, t)` (default: radius 118, `rot = t * 0.12`, sigil i at `i/12*2π + rot - π/2`).
- **Layer:** drawn in `ArenaView.drawZones`, under the aftermath marks, zones and every telegraph. The file wraps `ArenaView.drawZones` itself at load, so no other file changes; if the wrap is unwanted, add `if (typeof ULT_ECHO !== 'undefined' && this.liveRoom) ULT_ECHO.floor(ctx, this.liveRoom, t);` next to the `CL_FX.floor` line (a second call in a frame draws nothing).

## Rooms, weather, grade, camera (Wave 1, E4)

- **The act:** `roomev` `k: 'svact'` with `{ act }`, where `act` is 1–4 or `night`. It sets the colour grade (amber hall, steel forge, violet starlight, white-gold town fire, cool night blue) and the ash fall's density. In Act IV the ash becomes burning paper and cinders.
- **The camera:** `roomev` `k: 'svcam'` with `{ zoom, dur }` pulls the camera back (zoom below 1) or returns it to 1, eased over `dur`. Only Act IV uses it.
- **Slow motion is presentation only.** The host sim is paused through a set piece, and the client runs its effects at 40% speed for the moment a set piece names. *(The script sends `slow: [from, to]` inside the setpiece event; planned.)*

**Built (E4):** `client/js/sovereign-rooms.js` (`SV_ROOMS`), hooked in from its own file; `arena-view.js` is not edited.
- **Hooks:** it wraps `ArenaView.buildFloor` (the five ids), `drawAmbient` and `roomEvent` (reads `svact` and `svcam`; every message still reaches the original `roomEvent`).
- **Order each frame:** flat colour beyond the floor canvas, the grade (a multiply and a faint additive wash over the floor), the room's own life (embers, flames, seams breathing), the ash fall. All of it is under telegraphs and units.
- **Rooms:** a circle the size of `ARENA.r` (there is no `ARENA_DEFS` entry yet, so it is the default 520; add `sovereign` and `sovereign:<form>` entries to `shapes.js` to choose another). The margin is 300 to 340, and 440 for the square and the night so the Act IV pull-back stays on drawn ground.
- **The act and the camera reset** each time the hall floor is built (a new pull). The camera zoom only applies while the current floor is a Sovereign room.
- **Camera hook for `main.js` (one line):** in the arena camera (`zoom = clamp(Math.min(W, H) / 960, 0.55, 1.1) * camOut();`) multiply by `(typeof SV_ROOMS !== 'undefined' ? SV_ROOMS.camZoom(now) : 1)`. Leave `camOut()` itself alone: the wheel reads it to save the setting.
- **Positions the art leaves for the script:** the forge's three scorched spots are 120 degrees apart at 0.52 of the radius (angles -90, 30 and 150 degrees, so one is straight up). The hall's braziers stand on the lip at 45, 135, 225 and 315 degrees.
- **Preview:** open `tools/preview/sovereign-rooms.html` in a browser (it loads the game files by relative path, so open it from the repo). Query options: `?room=0..4` (one room large), `&act=1..4|night`, `&t=seconds`, `&tells=0`. `window.__svRoomPreview(canvas, form, act, t, { tells })` draws one room.

- **The first answer** is the first echo of the pass: Grubnak's plates, Skitterra's threads, Volkar's charges, the Hollow King's Doom. It is never turned. Its sigil flares: roomev `svsignal:n { name 'The First Signal', who, x, y, turn, at, dur }` (`at`: seconds from the message until the echo stands and the sigil flares; 0.5 for the Hollow King, who already stands at the portal). `client/js/sv-signal.js` draws it (a gold ring and a finer one, a four-pointed glint, a few embers; about 2 s, over the room like `sovereign-fx.js`, a pure function of the clock). No text, no painted ground.
- **What is turned** (`callThree(list, then, turn)` now hands `then(ns, ats)`; `turnPt` and `framePt` turn a point, `framePt` nudging by up to 24 degrees to clear a hole): the second and third echoes stand on their sigils turned (`ats`); the dance's `rot` is its old value plus the angle; pass one: the dive's three spots (`diveShort`, `T`) and Corvane's place and lance origin (`lanceTwo`'s `C`); pass two: Hrimgar's glass patch (`glassPatch(ns, at)`, the nearest sheet is the foot if the turned place falls between sheets) and Brannoc's throw, from where he stands, aimed by `wheelPlan(at, T)`; pass three: the four walls turn together (`twinRot`, read by `wallPoint` and `cornersFor`, and sent as `wall`; the client already drew the wall from `ev.wall`) and are reset after the pass; pass four: the three pillars (`pillarSpots(T)`). Pass one has no turn, so it is the base frame the others are read against.
- **Holes:** every turned place still goes through `pickFree` or a hole test that was already there (dive spots, pillars, the wall, the wheel's catch ring), and the echoes are nudged off holes. Checked with a probe that dug four extra random holes in Act IV on top of the two practice ones (12 runs, post5 bots): no plate, dive landing, pillar, wheel catch ring or twin corner ever fell inside a hole, and no turned echo stood in one. The unturned echoes (the first of a pass, and Corvane in pass one) sometimes stand over a hole, as before the turn, and a twin wall's centre was inside one in 2 of 7 cases (its 150 wide circle is never covered by a hole), as before.
- **The client** takes every turned position from the message (svflambe, svdive, svwheel, svglass, svpillar, svtwin, svdance, svecho, svlance already did); nothing in `sovereign-fx.js` or `ult-echo.js` needed changing. The bots read telegraphs only, so `bots.js` is untouched.
- **Sound:** recipe `sv-signal` (glass note, octave, a thin rise, a small bell; levelled with `render.js --write-trim`), played by `sound-hooks.js` on `svsignal` as the sigil flares. There is no `sfx-map.js` entry (it is not a cast).
- **Journal:** page `The First Signal` for all 23 heroes (`journal/sovereign.js`). It is not a cast, so `main.js` tells the journal when `svsignal` arrives (`Journal.castStart` with the message's `name`, as for a cast, not in practice runs); `journal-lint` is clean.
- **Bots (post5, Act IV only, normal):** perfect bots 75% before (54 of 72) and 68% after (49 of 72); `HUMAN=dps` 45% before (76 of 168) and 51% after (86 of 168). Within the spread between seed sets; pass three still takes most wipes, and pass four took more wipes after (15 against 6 of 96 pulls).
- **Not done / notes.** Not seen in the live game: the flare was only checked as a function against a stub canvas, and the turned layout through a headless probe. A tidy second look at the angles is cheap: they are one line.

- **The move.** Cast `The Anvil Count` (3.6 s, anim `call`), then one tower per hero, each 7.4 s after the last: they can be soaked at 9.0, 16.4, 23.8 and 31.2 s from the cast, so about 32 s with four heroes. Each hero is given 1 to 4 pips (a shuffle, then the healer moved to 2 or 3 of 4, the middle of 3, the first of 2, so the healer never waits longest). The tower for pip k is laid 5.2 s before it can be soaked, on a whole forge sheet (never sunk or cracking, not within 150 of a live head or a stump, not within 200 of an earlier tower of the same count, inside radius 380): the nearest such sheet 320 to 620 from the hero to the point opposite them through the middle, with 400 added for every sunk sheet their straight run crosses. Only the tower in play exists; the later ones are not sent until their turn. A hero who has fallen gets no tower.
- **The tower.** `f.mech` type `tower`, circle r 85, `need` 1, tag `svAnvil`, `anvilFor` = the owner's id. Damage keys `anvil` 3000 (the soak) and `anvilFail` 8000 (a raidwide, 40% of a hero, not a wipe), both unscaled by the act (`actDmg`'s list). Inside by the owner: fine. Inside by anyone else without the owner, or nobody: `tower-fail` on everyone (the engine hits everyone when nobody stands in; `onResolve` lays it when someone else does). `f.sv.anvil` = `{ done, bad, skip }` for tools.

The wall waits for the count (`busy`).

**Built (Foretold), `sovereign.js` (`foretold`, `FORE`), `client/js/sv-foretold.js` (`SV_FORETOLD`, new), and small edits to `bots.js`, `sound-hooks.js`, `sfx-synth.js`, `sfx-map.js`, `journal/sovereign.js`.** Act III's dance block (Fate Projection), the course's 690 step (11:30); it replaces the lone `seedOne` at 695 (still in `f.moves`), and the 710 Kindling is unchanged.
- **The move.** Cast `Foretold` (anim `dance`, 6.0 s: `FORE.walk`). Four ghosts rise in three pockets round a point O between the middle and the Sovereign (at most 190 from the middle, so everyone stays near it). The tank (outer) and the healer (inner, 100 closer) take the blue share in one pocket, one DPS the red spread, the other the Seed (the next carrier after `sv.seedLast`, the usual rule). With three alive there is no spread; with two or fewer, no share. The layout is the first of 24 turns (15 degrees apart) that keeps every ghost 70 clear of a hole, 110 of the Sovereign and 80 inside the wall, nearest the Sovereign (the tank counts double); so it is the same every pull unless the holes differ.
- **The blades.** 3.4 s before landing (`FORE.lead`) three red cones from O (`svForeFan`, arc about 1.49 rad each, 0.6 rad gaps between them, `dmg.foreFan` 76000, lethal) are painted; the pockets are the gaps and nothing marks them. A hero outside a pocket is cut down.
- **The marks** land 1.0 s after the fans (`FORE.lag`), each following its hero until the fans land, then held: the share (`svForeShare`, a 'stack' of r 140 on the tank, `need` 2, 5200 total), the spread (`svForeSpread`, r 100, 2200) and the seed (`svForeSeed`, an 'aoe' of r 95, 2200, not avoidable, bursts through `burst()` where the ghost stood if its hero is within 45 of it, else where it locked). All three are small on purpose; the fan is the check. The keys are in `dmg` (`foreFan foreSpread foreSeed foreShare`), so `ACT_DMG` scales them (the marks are Normal's small numbers).
- **Net.** `roomev` `svfore:<n>` `{ g: [[heroId, x, y, kind (0 share, 1 spread, 2 seed), facing]], ox, oy, a: [fan middle angles], arc, fan (s to the fans), mark (s to the marks), lead, sr, hr, dur }`; a second `svfore:<n>` with no `g` ends it (the push into Act IV sends one). The seed also sends the usual `svseed:<n>` (the Seeds of Ash drawing on its carrier). Caelum's echo plays `attack` for 2.4 s.
- **Drawing.** `sv-foretold.js` (after `sovereign-fx.js` in `index.html`) wraps `ArenaView.drawTelegraphs` (under: the rift at O, a silver footing and the hero's own picture washed cold at 58% for each ghost, the blue bond between the two, a faint thread from each hero to their ghost, the local hero's brightest) and `drawRoomOver` (over: the mark above each head: a red burst ring, two linked blue rings, the split seed; a silver streak down each fan as it lands; a ring where each mark lands).
