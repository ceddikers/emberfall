# Skitterra rework (2026-10-25)

Goal: lift Queen Skitterra to 8+/10 on Normal and Hard. A review found: Act I is ten single moves with 14 to 15 s of calm between them (the weakest pacing in set 1); the Dark Between leaves her untargetable for 30 s with nothing for the tank and melee to do; Hard's Eightfold Lunge only shows its floor about two seconds ahead (six steps, 1.15 s apart); Snare Weave's pockets read poorly; Hard stacks a second hunt drop 2.3 s behind the first in the dark, where it is unreadable. Bots: Normal 30/30 at `GEAR=fresh` (too easy), Hard 63% at `GEAR=mid` (target 45 to 60%).

## Finding a fight's idea (Skitterra's answers)

1. **What is happening in this room?** A broodmother's hunting web, hung between eight pillars. She has laid her eggs in it, strung her threads across it and sits at the hub. The party is the prey that came into her larder. By the end the web itself is giving way.
2. **What question does the fight keep asking?** "What do I break?"
3. **The rule.** Everything she has built can be cut, and what you cut changes what she can do to you. A clutch broken is two fewer spiderlings later; a knot cut is one less drop in the dark, but a strip of floor gone at the end; a pillar you hide behind is chipped by every Acid Deluge. So the rule explains moves the party has not met: if it is in the web, it can be broken, and it was worth something.
4. **The warning.** Her legs light in order before the Lunge, and (new) the whole lunge is drawn on the floor from the first moment as a faint chain, each strike stronger as it nears (the ghost chain). The eggs carry their own hatch clock, the threads and knots stand in plain view, a ring of floor shudders for six seconds before it goes. Every telegraph is on the floor, on both difficulties.
5. **Acts.** I. The Web (one hunt, now in movements: a move and the one that answers it), The Dark Between (the seam: lights out, the brood stirs, she drops), II. The Brood (pairs, then the Swarm), III. The Collapse (the Chrysalis, then the floor falls ring by ring to nothing but the hub's last disc and the silk).
6. **How it ends.** On the last of the silk: the party stands on spokes and threads with the pit all round, while she drops, reels and lunges across a floor that is no longer there to dodge on. Then Devour, or she falls into her own web.
7. **What Hard changes.** Six-step Lunges (and a faint chain of all six first), green and red Silk Lines, a burning beam on every hatched pair, three spiderlings to a clutch, two drops per uncut thread (now 3.6 s apart, not 2.3), Molt, Ceiling Ambush, Snare Weave, sticky acid, and her three moves: Egg Sacs, Fang Rush, Spinneret Volley.
8. **Where luck comes in, and how it is read.** The farthest hero is hunted (the party controls it by standing close), the cocoon falls on a random one (the circle follows them until 1.6 s out), the Lines pick two at random. Nothing lands without a circle first.
9. **Tank and healer.** The tank takes Silk Wrap (rooted where it lands, so the Lunge that follows has to be survived from that spot) and, in the dark, holds whatever hatches; the healer gets Venom Mist, the Reel and the Collapse's falls.
10. **Her own.** A boss whose room you dismantle: the party's choices about what to break are the fight, and they pay out later (dark drops, Swarm size, floor at the end).
11. **What players will talk about.** The Dark Between (lights out, the brood hatching around the tank, a drop from nowhere), cutting a knot just before she runs the thread, the last silk.

## What changes

**Act I, now seven movements.** The ten single moves become seven sets in which a move is followed by the one that answers it, with 10 to 12 s of calm between sets and 5 to 8 s inside: Mist into Silk Wrap; Brood into Silk Reel (Hard adds Egg Sacs); Spin into Strand Reel (Hard adds Fang Rush); Venom Spit into a Lunge fought across the pools; Mist into the Deluge; the Wrap-Lunge into a second Spit; Lines into Mist. The two Silk Wraps are about 90 s apart. The length stays about the same (the script has to outlast a slow party).

**The Dark Between.** The eggs do not wait while she is up there: each time, the clutch with the shortest clock splits and a spiderling (on Hard, a linked pair with its beam) runs at the tank. The brood clock shows the clutch's countdown. The tank holds it, the melee cut it down, the ranged go to the knots and the eggs; a clutch broken first sends nothing. Hard's drops are spread out: a thread's two drops are 3.6 s apart, and the hunt's second circle only appears when the first has landed.

**Hard Lunge.** All six steps are on the floor from the cast, the next in full and the later ones faint, as with Grubnak's Double Chop. Normal shows its four the same way.

**Snare Weave.** The pockets are wider (radius 100, was 75), so the clear floor reads as clear floor and the whole party fits in one.

**The Collapse, a climax.** Denser (nothing quiet for more than about 10 s); the Reel, Lines and Lunge are now fought on thinning silk; a fourth ring takes the hub down to a disc (215 across the middle) for the last stretch; and the hunt returns as the finale: From the Dark, from the web overhead, falls on whoever stands farthest out on the silk, then the next, three times in turn (four on Hard). No new cast name, so no new sound.

Unchanged: telegraphs never hidden (red damage, blue share), 8 to 10 minutes, one named enrage (Devour) with about a minute of slack (about 45 s on Hard), Hard has six moves Normal never uses (no more added), no HP-gate pushes or walls in set 1.

## Results (bots)

Normal at `GEAR=fresh`: about 90% (81/90 over three seed sets of 30), was 30/30. Hard at `GEAR=mid`: about 62% (56/90), was about 63%, with a fourth ring, the finale hunt and a new dark to play. Base-gear line-ups had to be eased (fall 15600, Mist 10300, Brood spiderlings 20000, a second Silk Wrap kept 90 s from the first so the tank's cooldown is back) so each still clears Skitterra in check.js; the Tidecaller line-up (about a quarter at base gear) now gets twelve tries. Two fixes were needed on the way: the Brood now starts with her walking to the hub, and hatchlings come out on the inward side of the clutch (the threads run past the clutches on their far side, and the bots would not cross one to reach what hatched).

## Cool-factor pass (2026-10-26)

Goal: signature moves that look and feel spectacular, dodges that feel earned, and a Collapse Reel that is a skill check with an answer. No new cast names (no new sounds), no change to the script's order, Hard's own moves unchanged.

**Looks and feel (`client/js/skitterra-fx.js`, SK_FX, called from boss-vfx.js `impact`).** An Eightfold Lunge strike is now a jointed chitin leg that comes down the cone and drives into the stone (a red-pink claw, a ring at the puncture, flying fibres, a web of cracks); Strand Reel's strand twangs like a plucked string when it goes taut; From the Dark and Ceiling Ambush land in a star of snapped threads. Hit-stop and a camera push on the big landings (Lunge, Deluge, Fang Rush, Silk Wrap, the drops, never more than once in a short while). The whiff: a hero who stood within about 80 px outside a Lunge cone, a Spit cone, a Strand or Fang lane, a drop circle or a glob when it landed (and was not hit) gets the leg's tip, a glob or a strand cracking past: a streak, fibres or venom droplets, a ring at their feet, and for your own hero a flash, a camera push and a soft whoosh. It is only drawn for a hero the blow missed, so it rewards a dodge and never shows where is safe.

**The Collapse Reel.** The finding: the Reel itself almost never put anyone in the pit (1 fall in 29 while hooked). Most reel-related falls were the snap: a bot snapped by dashing straight away from her, which on the thinned floor is off the silk. Two changes. (1) The silk now stops dead at the brink of fallen floor: the drag is a straight line toward her, it never carries you over a gap, and where it stops the thread goes taut and shudders (no bite, you are slowed to a crawl while hooked, so dashing along the silk to snap it is still the answer). (2) The drag's line is on the floor from the moment the hook lands: pale chevrons running toward her along the exact path, and where the floor has gone, a red anchor bar at the lip with a short dashed run on past it (the path, never a safe spot). Players already chose their dash direction; bots now snap along a heading that lands on floor (back along the drag first), as a player would. Code: `SK_FX.pullStep` in main.js's drag, the same rule in raid-host.js for bots (`room.safe`), `drawReels` in boss-tells.js for the line.

**Numbers.** Those two changes alone lifted Hard from about 57-60% to about 70% at `GEAR=mid` (63/90), so Hard's Eightfold Lunge strike is 14400 (was 13300; the biggest killer of the fight, and the one a dodge answers). Normal unchanged.

**Results (bots).** Hard `GEAR=mid` (seeds 1000, 2000, 3000 of 30): 48/90 (53%) at Lunge 14400; 50/90 at 14500 and 59/90 at 14200 bracket it, so the real rate sits around 55-60%. Normal `GEAR=fresh`: 79/90 (88%). Clear time about 9:00 on Hard (Devour lands at 576 s, so about 35 s of slack on a mean clear), about 7:20 on Normal. The check.js base-gear Skitterra line-ups all clear.

## Tank move: Fang Strike (2026-10-26)

Goal: the tank waited 30 to 60 s between things of their own (the Wraps cost about 80% of the tank's health and cannot come more often). One new move for the tank, on both difficulties, lighter than the Wrap, to fill the waits.

**The move.** She rears and drives a fang down a lane from her through the tank. The lane is drawn from the cast (a plain red lane, 150 wide, from her through where the tank stood when it began); whoever is in it when it lands takes it, the same for anyone behind the tank. So the tank's job is to step out of it (it is a short cast: 1.8 s Normal, 1.5 s Hard), and the party's is to keep out of the strip behind the tank. It follows the fight's own rule: she shot a line to the one who holds her, and what stands behind him is in it. Anim `fang`, tag `fangstrike`, sound `bm-skitterra-fang-strike` (scratchpad newmoves/skitterra.md). About 8000 on Normal, 7200 on Hard (9000 with Hard's 1.25: x1.125 Normal), a quarter of the tank's health, so it never needs a cooldown. No painted safe spot, nothing hidden.
Hard sharper: faster cast, and in the Collapse a second lane follows the first the moment it has landed (1.4 s warning), set on wherever the tank stepped to dodge; on thinning silk the dodge has a cost.

**Why static, not tracking.** A lane that follows the tank until it sets was tried first (the way Venom Spit follows its two targets): the bots' read for a line aimed at them walks the tank out to the wall to bait it, with the boss in tow (the tank sat 450 px out at every Strike). A lane drawn once, where the tank stands when the cast begins, plays as it should (the tank steps a few paces aside; the bots stay in melee).

**Where it sits.** In the quiet stretches, as the tail of a set or between the two steps of one, never within about 11 s of a Wrap in either direction (checked: at least 11.1 s after, 11.4 s before on Normal; 13.3 and 12.7 on Hard), and each follows an act's own pace (the sets' calm before it was trimmed by what the Strike takes, so each act's script is as long as it was: Normal 149 / 36 / 213 / 117 s against 152 / 36 / 213 / 114; Hard 188 / 41 / 220 / 128 against 187 / 41 / 202 / 121, the Hard Act II set pairs being kept in order). Normal: eleven a fight (Act I four, Act II five, Act III two); Hard: fourteen (Act I five, Act II seven, Act III two, those with a second lane). In Hard Act II the Strikes sit on the sets that end in a Mist, a Spit, a Fang Rush or a Deluge, and after the Lunge sets; the order of every set is unchanged (the Swarm, the Snare Weave and the Spit sets keep their places). The last set of an act never carries one (the next act's first cast would run over it), and none comes in the Dark.

## Lunge readability, closing loops and Mist/Lunge variety (2026-10-27)

The user's play of the live version: the Lunge floor was hard to read (every leg's telegraph showed at once), and the Mist/Lunge section felt like two moves on repeat with no time to hit her. Findings: the "two moves over and over" was almost certainly the closing loops: a party that could not finish an act's sets met `[Mist, Lunge]` again and again, +10% a pass, every 22 s.

**Lunge drawing (client only, Skitterra's `lunge` tag, her husk's replay too; Grubnak's chains and every other boss unchanged).** The strike landing next is the only full telegraph (the wash, the pink tear, the rim). The strike after it is a thin dashed outline, no fill, so the turn direction reads; later strikes are not on the floor until they are second in line (her lit legs still show the whole order). Each strike is on the floor at least two steps before it lands: 2.6 s on Normal (1.3 s steps), 2.0 s on Hard (1.0 s steps), so the 1.2 s rule holds.
