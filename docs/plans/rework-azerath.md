# Azerath rework: the rune you can read, a fairer Hard, and the sigils taught before the finale

*2026-10-25. Review: 7 out of 10 fun on Normal, 6 out of 10 on Hard for fairness. The Rune Pair ("together or apart by element") and the Binding (the tank leads elementals to sigils) are a distinctive pair of jobs and Convergence is clear. What held it back: the rune-to-element rule was learned, not read; Hard stacked a Mark, a Swap, the spell and a Counterspell inside about twenty seconds; the fire and void rings could one-shot a bot; and Normal bots cleared 12 of 12 at `GEAR=post1`. Goal: fun 8 or more, Hard fair and tuned (target 50 to 60% at `GEAR=post2`).*

## His answers to "Finding a fight's idea" (docs/DESIGN.md)

1. **What is happening in this room?** A tower drowned to its windows, and its master still at his desk in the middle of it. The floor is seven circles of old spellwork (a middle one, two rings and four sigils for fire, frost, lightning and the void), and he works his spells through them without ever walking.
2. **What question does the fight keep asking?** "Which element is it, and who holds the rune?"
3. **What is the rule a player learns?** Fire and the void part the two bearers; frost and lightning bring them together. The floor now says which element is live: when the Mark lands, the sigil of the coming spell's element lights, and the runes themselves are drawn to match the rule (a closed ring for the two that join, a broken ring for the two that part). The same sigils are the ones the Binding sends the elementals to and the ones Convergence lights at the end.
4. **How does the fight warn?** The Mark's link and glyphs over the two bearers; the lit sigil; the spell named in the feed; then the spell's whole shape on the floor from the first moment of its cast (circles on each bearer, a blue circle between them, a bolt joining them), following the bearers until a moment before it lands.
5. **Acts.** Fire, Frost, the Binding (the seam), Lightning, Void, Convergence. HP gates push the fight on; no wall.
6. **Ending.** Convergence: all four sigils at once, each in its own element, with the reaction filling the lens where two meet. It is the sigils the party has learned all fight, lit together. This rework adds **Sigil Flare** in Acts III and IV, two neighbouring sigils lit at once with the lens between them, so the finale is the same shape, bigger.
7. **What does Hard change?** Casts 15% faster, and Counterspell (the last spell's areas come again on the far side), Rune Swap (the runes jump to two other heroes) and Arcane Mark (a stack where one hero stood). It shuffles each act's middle sets, and Sigil Flare lights the opposite pair after the first (cross the middle).
8. **Luck.** Who gets the runes and which two elements they carry are random, but the spell's element is shown on the floor from the Mark on. Sigil Flare's pair is random and painted in full.
9. **Tank and healer.** The tank takes the Arcane Lance and leads each elemental to its sigil and holds it there (the Binding); the healer gets the Arcane Burst raidwides and the rings' chip damage.
10. **Its own.** The only fight where a pair of players is tied together and the rule for how to stand is set by a spell's element, with the room's own sigils naming the element.
11. **What will players talk about?** The Rune Pair, the Binding, and "which sigil is lit".

## Changes

1. **The live element is on the floor (both difficulties).** The Mark lights the sigil of the coming spell's element until the spell lands (roomev `live`). The rune over each bearer's head is drawn by its element's rule: fire and the void as a broken ring, frost and lightning as a closed one. The rune of the live element is bigger and bright, the other dim. No teaching text; the rule is still the player's to learn.
2. **The Hard chain stays, with room to breathe (Hard).** Mark, Swap, spell and Counterspell are the fight's best layered sequence (the same kind of thing as the Hollow King's graves, Writ and Crown), so they stay one sequence. What changes is the spacing: the Counterspell now waits a little longer after the spell, echoes at most three shapes (it used to be five) and hits softer.
3. **A longer window after the Swap (Hard).** The spell now begins about 5.4 s after the Swap cast starts (was 3.6 s); the new bearers have about six seconds from the jump to the lock.
4. **Hard rings trimmed.** The fire and void rune rings, the reaction circle, the echo and the Pyre Rings hit for about 65 to 80% of what they did, so one ring leaves a hero standing at full health; two still kill. The Hard Arcane Lance is the same as Normal's (a tank at three quarters health was dying to a Lance). No two Arcane Bursts open back to back when a pass runs long (the closing passes of Acts I and IV start with the Lance).
5. **Sigil Flare (new, both difficulties; Hard adds a second pair).** Two neighbouring sigils light and blast together, and the lens between them blooms (the same reaction as Convergence). On Hard the opposite pair follows a moment later, so the safe half becomes the next blast and the party crosses the middle. It replaces a filler Arcane Burst in Acts III and IV. New cast, so a new sound (`bm-archmage-sigil-flare`), an icon and a journal entry per hero.

## Kept

Telegraphs never hidden (every shape is on the floor from the cast), 8 to 10 minutes, one enrage (The Tide Rises casts at 10:00 / 10:05 and lands 8 s later, a minute of slack on Normal and about 45 s on Hard), HP-gate pushes and no wall, tank and healer gaps under about 10 s, Hard has Counterspell, Rune Swap and Arcane Mark that Normal never uses.

## Hard tuning pass (2026-10-26)

Hard `GEAR=post2`, plain line-up, was 87% (78 of 90); now 62% (56 of 90, seeds 1000/2000/3000). Normal `GEAR=post1` 87 of 90.

- **Tighter, not softer.** On Hard the Pyre Rings follow one another closer (2.3 s, was 2.8), the Leaping Bolt hops faster, the Rime Rune circle is a little smaller, the Sigil Flare's second pair follows sooner, and two bearers of a split rune must now stand further apart (and the reaction between them is bigger). The Counterspell lands sooner (a run across the room) and hits a little harder.
- **The echo of the echo (Hard).** After the far-side copy goes off, the first two shapes come back to where they began, so the old ground is not safe either. A new line in every hero's Counterspell journal entry.
- **Numbers.** The Hard Arcane Burst is 19200 (was 17000); the Rime Rune and Arcane Mark blows are a tenth stronger. Bots dodge every shape perfectly, so the bite that moves their clear rate is the unavoidable damage.

## Cool-factor pass (2026-10-26)

Look and feel only: no timing, damage or shape changed, so the rates above stand (re-measured below), and no journal line changed because the counterplay did not.

- **The whiff.** A hero a blow just missed, standing within a stride of its edge, gets the edge going past: a streak of the element along it, a small rune ring at their feet, sparks, and for your own hero a faint flash, a push of the camera and a soft whoosh. Drawn only for a hero the blow did not hit and was close to (never for a share, a tankbuster or a raidwide), so it is a reward after the fact and tells nothing about where is safe. Counterspell echoes whiff too, in pale violet.
- **The last beat of a wait.** Inside the red, a ring of the element closes on the middle of each circle (or sweeps in from the rim of a band, or a spearhead of light runs down a line) and lands at the instant the blow does. It times the landing; it paints nothing outside the outline. The rune bearers' feet get a tightening ring in the last moments before their spell.
- **The sigil stamp.** Every landing now slams the tower's own sigil onto the floor (a brass ring, the element's rune marks and its sign opening out and fading). Convergence and Sigil Flare run beams between the sigils through the middle of the floor and a star of light over him; the Pyre Rings run a ring of rune marks across the band; the lens burns a white seam along its length; the Counterspell echoes fold in on themselves with the runes turning the wrong way and pale shards flung out.
- **Screen feel.** Big landings push the camera and hold the frame an instant, with a flash in the element's colour, never more than once in a couple of seconds (your own view only).
- **The Binding.** A bound elemental's rune ring closes on its sigil with its light climbing; one left unbound tears a ring through the room.

## Skill pass (2026-10-26)

Gameplay half of the fun-and-complexity ask: dodges that are earned, not stumbled into. No new cast names (so no new sound or icon); two existing moves gained a second problem, both difficulties, every shape on the floor from the moment it is laid.

- **Ice Lances, the weave.** The moment the first lines lock on their heroes, a second volley is laid either side of each line and lands a beat after the first (Normal about 1 s, Hard a little less; Hard also has a third line to start with). The ground between a line and its flanks is safe through both, and so is the first line once it has fired. A sloppy side-step lands in a flank. Journal: every hero's Ice Lances entry rewritten.
- **Rime Rune, dodge then close in.** A lance out of him through each bearer lands a beat before the blue circle (Hard a hair tighter). The bearers have to leave their own lines and still come together, and everyone else has to be off both lines and then in the circle. Journal: every hero's Rime Rune entry rewritten.
- **Numbers.** Bots dodge every shape, so these moves do not move their clear rate; the Normal Arcane Burst goes 20200 to 20900 to bring Normal toward range. It cannot go much higher: line-up B (squishy, short on damage) clears about 58% before this pass and 44% at 20900, and a bigger Burst (21800) took it to a miss in check.js. Hard unchanged otherwise.
- **Rates** (30 runs per seed, seeds 1000/2000/3000): Normal `GEAR=post1` 83 of 90 (92%, was 97%; 78 of 90 = 87% at a Burst of 21800, which line-up B cannot take); Hard `GEAR=post2` 51 of 90 (57%, was 62%).

## Fix (2026-10-26, review of sets 2 and 3)

Rune Swap never reached "two other heroes" in a party of four: with three damage dealers and two bearing runes, the fallback shuffled everyone, so the runes could land back on the same pair (about one in six) or on the tank. The new pair is now the other damage dealer and then the tank, and only in a smaller party one of the old pair. Bots, Hard `GEAR=post2`: 50 of 90 (56%, was 57%).

## Fun pass (2026-10-26, set 2 and 3 fun pass)

Review asked for: something for the tank and healer to do in every act, an ending that peaks, a Hard that is read and not memorised, real choices. Boss file only; no new cast names, no new sounds.

- **Tank.** The Lance was the tank's only job: Normal Lances at about 28, 71, 126, 209, 293, 356, 464 s, so Acts II and IV had 80 to 108 s with nothing. Now the tank bears the runes that want the pair together (frost, lightning; every other one at least: when the last such spell left the tank out, the next brings it in, and with a Swap by sending the runes to two others first, as the Swap brings the tank in); every other Leaping Bolt starts on the tank; the Arcane Mark can rise beside the tank; Act IV has a second Lance (after the Rift Rune; Normal also before its last Burst). The stand-apart runes (fire, void) stay with the other heroes: a tank bearer made the bots lose a healer and a ranger to the circle (Normal fell to 17 of 30), and a bearer across the room is a hero the party can ill spare. Ice Lances stay off the tank (a hero beside the boss cannot get out of a line and its flanks). Longest stretch with no tank beat (Lance, Binding, bearer, Bolt start): Normal 86 s, now 71 s; Hard 70 s, now 68 s (24 seeds).
- **Healer.** The Bursts keep their places: a Burst takes most of a hero's health and moving them closer (tried: Acts I and III) sank the bots (Hard 1 of 30). So the long Hard Act II stretch with no Burst is not filled; its heal checks are the Arcane Mark and the Rime Rune (two stacks, kept 21 s or more apart). Longest stretch with no Burst, stack, Flare, Convergence, Counterspell or Binding pulse: Normal 82 s (unchanged), Hard 78 s to 77 s. Honest: the healer's gaps are about the same; the fight does not have more raidwides to move without making it harder.
- **Hard is read.** `mix` now also shuffles Act I's Lance and Pyre, Act II's and Act IV's Lance, and the finale's four middle sets; the openers and closers stay. An order is thrown back when two Lances or two Rune Marks sit side by side, a run of more than four sets has no Lance or Rune Mark, a run of more than three has no raidwide or stack, two Bursts come within 33 s, a Burst and a stack within 14 s, or two stacks within 20 s (the old shuffle could put the Mark next to the Rime Rune: Hard fell to a quarter). If no order passes in 80 draws the written order is used.
- **Ending.** The finale used to play the second Convergence and then a Mark or Ice Lances and a Leaping Bolt. It now ends on the second Convergence, with the other sets shuffled before it on Hard.
- **Bugs.** An act opened 2 to 4 s after the last cast of the one before began, cutting its bar (Pyre Rings by The Binding, 2.5 s; Burst by Ice Lances, Flare by Void Rifts, Burst by Convergence): act openers now wait 5 s. The header claimed Hard lays a third elemental into the Binding's pulses; both difficulties run three Calls and five pulses (comment fixed). Two Lances in neighbouring sets and Rune Marks side by side were possible in the Hard shuffle (now thrown back).
- **Hard script length.** Script ends 578 s (cast at 605, bots clear at about 566 s), as before. Shortening it to the 45 s slack by cutting every calm by a second made Hard much harder (post2 about 34%, post1 about 29%: the party meets the same hits in less time); a half-second cut was 48% / 40%; the third of a second kept is 56% / 42%. The slack is left as it was.
- **Rates** (30 runs per seed, seeds 1000/2000/3000): Normal `GEAR=post1` 82 of 90 (91%; 83 before); Hard `GEAR=post2` 50 of 90 (56%; 50 before); Hard `GEAR=post1` 38 of 90 (42%; 53 before, 59%, above its 40 to 45% target). Check line-ups: A 2 of 3 and B 3 of 3 on Normal; Hard smoke lasts to 294 s or more.
- **Not built (needs a new move):** a short tank-aimed follow-up to the Lance would fill the 70 s gaps better than any shuffle; it needs its own cast name, sound and journal line.

### Review round (2026-10-26)

- **The ending is now reachable.** Act V is two courses. The first runs to 6% of his health (a gate, so a fifth tick joins the bar at 0.06: `gates` lists it) and holds the Convergence, a sure Lance after it, then the shuffled rune sets (Hard) or rune, Burst, Ice Lances and Bolt (Normal). The second opens five seconds after the gate with the last Convergence, then the closing passes. In bot runs a clearing party meets Convergence three times: the opener, the 6% one, and the first closing pass (Normal 480 / 523 / 543 s, clear at 553 s; Hard 477 / 512 / 532 s, clear at 545 s). A Rune Mark whose spell was cut by a gate no longer leaves its pair on the bots' map (`unpair` at each act's start).
- **Tank beats counted for real.** The shuffle's tank-run check now counts only a Lance, a Rune Swap, a Rime or Storm Rune and the Binding. Longest stretch with no tank beat per act (Lance, Binding, a rune the tank bears, 24 seeds; before the pass, then now): Normal 44/83/46/90/80 to 44/72/47/72/53; Hard 63/69/58/78/39 to 69/57/57/68/57 (Act I grew because of the new set, below).
- **Hard Act I reads.** The Swap rune chain joined its shuffled sets; the act now has 19 distinct orders in 60 pulls (was 3), and Pyre Rings never sit in neighbouring sets. The check also sees the first closing step (no Lance into a closing Lance, no Burst into a closing Burst); Act III is the exception (its tail already ends on a Burst and a Flare). The rune-next-to-rune rule is gone: sets run one after the other, so it did not prevent a re-read at once.
- **Damage, Hard against Normal.** Pyre Rings: Normal 22300 to 15400, Hard stays 17000 (x1.10); Normal's Void self hit 11400 to 8000, Hard 8800 (x1.10). Measured with Normal's other numbers unchanged: no change in Normal's rate (56 of 60 either way). Not done: Arcane Burst (Hard 19200, Normal 20900) and Cinder Rune (Hard 16500, Normal 23900). Both are unavoidable hits on a squishy hero and the clear rate stands on a knife edge: Hard Burst 21000 gave 0 and 1 of 30 on post2 (23000 and Cinder 26300: 0 of 30 twice), the healer dying to the Burst that follows the Binding; and lowering Normal's Cinder to 15000 (so Hard would be x1.1) took Normal to 59 of 60. A lone step too short makes bots meet the closing passes' damage ramp before the gate: removing Act I's last Burst (and so 16 s of script) took Hard post2 from 56% to 34% on its own.

## Tank move: Circle Brand (2026-10-26)

The tank's longest waits were the worst in the game (Normal 44/72/39/59/53 s by act, Hard 69/57/37/56/50, the gap between one beat of his own and the next: a Lance, the Binding, a rune he bears, a Bolt that starts on him). New cast **Circle Brand**, both difficulties.

- **What it is.** The Rune Pair's rule for the shield alone. A rune of the act's element is laid on the tank (Fire in Act I, Frost II, Lightning III, the Void IV, any element in the finale): the orb takes the colour, the sigil lights, and the spell is drawn at his feet from the first moment of the cast, following him until a moment before it lands (1.4 s on Normal, 1.2 s on Hard). Fire and the void part: a ring of flame (the void's has a quiet middle) that the party keeps out of, so he walks it to where nobody stands. Frost and lightning join: a blue circle on him that at least one other must share, so he stays where the party can reach him. It is the same together-or-apart reading, drawn with shapes the party already knows (the Cinder and Rift rings, the Arcane Mark's circle), so a first sight is readable.
- **Why the tank's job.** It is aimed by where he stands, never by the clock: he picks the spot (clear of the party, or among them) and, for the ring, can step out of it after the lock. The party's part is small (keep out, or close in). Light by design: the tank takes 8000 (fire), 11000 + 5000 self (void) or 3600 shared + 6000 on him (stack), a fraction of the Lance (18600).
- **Where it sits.** Laid into the calm before another set (`brandIn`): its cast begins 6.4 s (Hard 5.7 s) after the cast before it, lands about 9 s later, and the set's own first cast keeps its place. Every cast start in the script is unchanged, so the script length (544 / 579 s), the Bursts and the Hard calm are as before. Nothing is added to the clock. Normal has 12 Brands (4/2/2/3/1 by act), Hard 15 (4/3/2/4/2). A Brand never opens straight after a Counterspell's echo and `mix` (Hard) throws back an order that puts one within 4 s of a Lance, 3 s of a Burst, 4 s of a stack, 8 s of another Brand, within 6 s before a Lance, or lets more than 40 s pass between the tank's own beats; the written order is valid for all of them.
- **Hard.** Shorter hold, a tighter circle (138 against 150, ring 135 against 125), 12% more damage (9000 against 8000 and so on), and in the frost act a lance of frost out of the boss through the tank lands a beat before the circle (the Rime Rune's trick: leave the line, then be where the party can close in).
- **Tried and dropped.** A Hard "relay" (the ring or the bolt jumps on to the hero nearest where it fell): it put an unavoidable hit on a squishy hero a few seconds before a Burst, and Hard fell to 42%. A heavier shared stack (14000 to 24000 in total): the chip before a Cinder Rune or a Burst killed the ranger; Normal fell to 83%. The stack is now mostly the tank's own hit.
- **Numbers** (24 seeds in a scratch timeline replay, longest stretch between the tank's own beats per act, counting a rune he bears from its Mark): Normal 44/72/39/59/53 to 25/33/20/24/28 (Act II is counted from the Mark, so the true gap is about 25); Hard 69/57/37/56/50 to 33/28/27/28/30. Bots, 30 runs per seed, seeds 1000/2000/3000: Normal `GEAR=post1` 83 of 90 to 84 of 90 (93%; 75 of 90 with the first, heavier stack); Hard `GEAR=post2` 53 of 90 to 48 of 90 (53%). Hard `GEAR=post1` seed 1000: 18 of 30. Line-ups A and B (12 runs): 8 and 9 clears.
- **Hard parity not changed.** The Brand adds no damage to the party, so the Hard Arcane Burst and Cinder Rune stay lighter than Normal's: the knife edge there is the healer's chip before the Burst, and a Brand that costs the party anything made it worse.
