# The October rework (agreed with the user, 2026-10-19)

The user's group are expert MMO raiders. This rework makes Emberfall harder and gives players more to work towards.
The rules in docs/DESIGN.md still apply unless this file changes them:
- No on-screen text telling players what to do. Information is fine: cast names, timers, threat numbers.
- In-game tooltips only (`Tip` / `data-tip`, never `title=`).
- Small numbers. A 1,000 crit is a big moment.
- Refined, non-"AI" menus: one warm palette, hairline frames, small-caps headings, square-ish corners, no emoji, no stacked glows.
- The user paints all art from written briefs (docs/art/*.md). New items need a brief line in docs/art/item-icons.md, and they fall back to a code-drawn icon until the art arrives.

Work happens on separate branches that get merged. Stay inside your area; if you must touch a shared file (main.js, fight.js, save.js, style.css), keep the edits small and self-contained so the merge stays easy. `node tools/check.js` must pass at the end.

---

## 1. Bosses: harder Normal, new Hard, no teaching text (branch `boss-rework`)

- **No teaching framing.**
  - Remove the boss panel's "Teaches" and "Favors" boxes (data.js `teaches` / `favors`, rendered in main.js around the boss panel).
  - Rewrite every blurb as flavour only. The "first raid, a fair teacher" kind of line goes.
  - Remove "teaching" or "tutorial" wording from boss file headers and DESIGN.md.
  - The Normal/Hard picker text may describe the mode factually, with no promise that the fight is gentle.
- **Normal is challenging from boss 1.** The user asked for "40% tight-but-learnable, 60% prog-raid". A good group should expect several wipes on its first night with any boss, Grubnak included.
  - Levers: harder-hitting mechanics; Vulnerability that really matters; tighter enrages; Normal runs the overlapping patterns from loop 1 (today some only start on loop 2); faster cadence.
  - One mistake should hurt a lot, and two should usually kill.
  - Everything stays fair: every Normal attack is telegraphed and readable.
- **Hard adds new things, not just bigger numbers.**
  - Each boss gets 2-3 brand-new Hard-only abilities or patterns: new casts with their own telegraphs, tells and impact VFX, in the same style as the boss's existing ones.
  - These come on top of the existing Hard modifiers. Hard can keep hiding some floor tells (the boss's body tells them instead).
  - Hard must stay fair and learnable.
- **Upcoming moves feed.** Every scripted step in each boss gets a player-facing name (the cast name). The host computes the next 2-3 named steps of the current phase and broadcasts them about once a second, and whenever they change:
  - Message: `{ t: 'bnext', l: [[name, secondsUntil], ...] }`.
  - A phase change may cut the list short. That's fine.
  - The raid-info branch draws it. Just emit it.
- **Bots** must cope with the new mechanics. `bots.js` regex-matches cast names for defensives, so keep those lists in step.
- **Checks.**
  - Normal bot line-ups should still clear sometimes when bots play well; aim for at least 1 of 3, and improve bot play rather than softening bosses.
  - Add one Hard smoke run per boss: no errors, and the fight lasts at least 90 s.
- Per-boss numbers live in the boss files and journals, not in DESIGN.md; DESIGN.md only gets the boss's row in its stats table.

## 2. Items, loot, gold (branch `items-rework`)

- **Rarities, colours set by the user:** Common (grey, starter gear only), Uncommon **blue**, Rare **purple**, Legendary **orange**, Mythic **red**.
- **Rarity is a property of the item.** A rarity is a different set of items, not the same item at a higher tier.
- **No ladder between bosses.** A Grubnak Legendary is as strong as a Hollow King Legendary. Bosses differ in themes, effects and party bonuses.
- **Power by rarity.**
  - Uncommon: a clean stat line.
  - Rare: stats plus a small unique effect.
  - Legendary: a strong unique effect. Class items with ability enhancements live here.
  - Mythic: a build-defining effect.
  - Stat budget grows by rarity: about 1.0 / 1.15 / 1.3 / 1.45.
- **Effects** use a small, data-driven hook set in the combat engine, e.g. on crit, on ogcd use, on heal, while above or below an HP fraction, after a dash, cooldown reduction, or resource gain. Effects are modest; gear stays roughly +15-25% best-in-slot, so fights stay about mechanics. HP and defence stay host-side (sanitized in the gear message).
- **Loot tables, per boss.** Each boss has its own table across the four rarities:
  - Its general items.
  - Its class items: weapon and armour for each class.
  - 2 new Mythics per boss.
  - Reuse existing items and names where they fit; add new ones with brief lines.
- **A clear drops a chest:** 4 items on Normal, 5 on Hard.
  - Each item rolls its rarity first (Normal about Uncommon 55 / Rare 30 / Legendary 12 / Mythic 3; Hard about 30 / 40 / 22 / 8), then picks an item of that rarity from the boss's table.
  - Class-locked items can only roll for classes that a human in the run is playing.
- **Need / Greed / Pass.**
  - After the results, each item appears as a card with Need (only if your hero can use it), Greed and Pass, and a ~25 s timer that auto-passes.
  - The host rolls 1-100: the best Need roll wins, otherwise the best Greed. Results go to chat, e.g. "Ava won [Item] (Need 87)".
  - Bots don't roll.
  - If everyone passes, the item is salvaged into a little gold for each human.
- **Gold.**
  - Wallet is account-wide (save.js). The amount shows in the inventory and somewhere quiet on the town HUD.
  - Gold per clear, small numbers: Normal Grubnak 40, Skitterra 45, Volkar 50, Hollow King 60; Hard ×2. Wipes give nothing. Trials give nothing.
  - Items can be sold in the inventory: Common 2, Uncommon 8, Rare 15, Legendary 30, Mythic 60.
- **Save:** bump the save version with an upgrade step. Old items must load, never be lost, and map sensibly; the legacy Heroic `q` flag needs handling.
- Update the inventory tooltips (rarity colour and name, effect text) and DESIGN.md (the gear section).

## 3. Talents (branch `talents`, after items)

- Each hero has **5 rows × 3 talents**. You hold one pick per row at a time.
- **Rows unlock in order with gold:** 60 / 120 / 200 / 300 / 400.
- **Reveal:** you can read the next row you could unlock; rows beyond it show as sealed shapes with no text. Unlocking reveals new parts of your kit. Unlocked rows stay readable.
- **Shared across specs; free respec in town any time.** A talent on a move that a spec replaces applies to the replacement (match `replaces`).
- **Row themes**, roughly:
  1. Rotation.
  2. Survival or utility.
  3. The class gauge / resource.
  4. Big cooldowns.
  5. Capstone: a new part of an ability the player didn't know it had.
- **Balance.** Each talent is about +2-4% output or a comparable utility gain. A full set should be at most about +15%. Picks within a row should be close in value but play differently. The simulator and checks stay balanced; base-kit balance is checked without talents.
- **Where:** a Talent trainer NPC in town (and a hotkey), opening a refined panel.
- Talents are kit-level effects checked like perks (`k.talent(id)`). The host doesn't need them unless one touches HP or defence; keep those out.
- Bots take no talents.

## 4. Raid info and threat (branch `raid-info`)

- **Taunt for every tank, required by the user.**
  - An off-GCD taunt on its own key. Pick a key that doesn't clash, show it on the hotbar, and give it a tooltip.
  - Effect: your threat becomes the current highest +10%, and the boss fixes on you for ~3 s.
  - On adds: they attack you for ~6 s. Adds should use threat too, not just "nearest".
  - Cooldown ~20 s.
- **Real threat.** Tune the tank multiplier so a careless tank can actually lose the boss to a bursting DPS, with taunt as the answer. Clamp the `threat` field clients send.
- **Bot tanks** taunt back when they lose the boss or an add.
- **Threat meter:** a compact list near the party frames, showing each player's threat as a share of the top (the tank at 100%). Highlight your own row, and warn quietly as a non-tank nears the tank's threat. Clients already receive per-player threat in `fps` (index 10, currently thrown away).
- **Upcoming boss moves:** draw the `bnext` feed (see section 1) under the boss cast bar: the next 2-3 names with countdowns. Hide it when there's no data.
- **Boss buffs and debuffs:** icons under the boss bar with tooltips. Include Vulnerability / Hunter's Mark, damage up, enrage, and your own DoTs and brands on the boss (poison, Thornlash, acid stacks, the Inquisitor's brand).
