# Set 2: the wild realm beyond the road

*2026-10-22, rewritten after the user's pick (Wyrm, Frost Titan, Archmage, Hydra, and a maybe on the Void Gate). A design pass, not code. Nothing here is built.*

Set 1 walked the King's Road through the court's own halls (kitchen, silver caves, beacon, Barrow). The road does not end at the Barrow: past it the court's land gives way to the wild realm — the old things the kings pushed back from the valley. Each boss of set 2 holds one of the realm's great places. The tone is high fantasy: dragons, giants, sorcery, monsters.

## Rules every boss here keeps (the user's standing design notes)

- Telegraphs are never hidden on any difficulty. Red = damage, blue = stack/soak, safe spots are never painted.
- Choreographed and learnable, but it must **feel natural**: random targets (the Grubnak soak-marker model), moves aimed by where players stand or what they just did, never clock hacks or filler casts. Hard shuffles the dance each pull where a group could otherwise memorise it.
- Non-looping, 8–10 minutes, acts on HP gates, one named enrage with about a minute of slack (about 45 s on Hard), and no stretch where the tank or healer is idle for more than about 10 s.
- **The finale of the set is the hardest.**
- Hard is gear-gated and opens one boss at a time, as in set 1. Set 2 Normal opens after the Hollow King falls.
- Small numbers. No teaching text in the fight; the journal holds the knowledge.

## New systems for the set (small; each tried on Grubnak first)

1. **Visible HP gates:** tick marks on the boss bar where the acts change.
2. **Pushes:** reach a gate early and the boss moves on, skipping the scripted mechanics left in that act (the FF14 "phase push"). Slow parties still get the existing closing sequence.
3. **Walls:** a boss can be invulnerable at a gate until a scripted mechanic resolves, with a visible reason (a shield, a sealed crystal). Used sparingly.
4. The new mechanics feed the points system's existing `soaked`, `saves` and `perfects` so the awards stay meaningful.

## The bosses

### Boss V — Vhalgrath, the Ember Wyrm · "Tyrant of the Cinder Peaks"
- **Place:** a volcanic crater ringed with cooling lava and bone-white ribs of older kills.
- **Shape:** *the hunter in the air.* The fight alternates between **ground** and **flight**.
- **Question:** *where is it going to land?*
- **Acts (4):** Ground (claws, tail, breath cone) / First Flight / Ground, scorched / Final Flight into the Ember Heart.
- **Unique set piece — the Flight Path:** when it takes off, a faint trail of embers draws the *line it will dive along* over 4 s, and the party reads the path and stays off it. The dive's landing point is random among three crater spots, shown by the scorch marks that bloom before it lands.
- **Lingering floor:** each breath leaves molten ground for 20 s; where players stand during the ground act decides how much floor is left for the air acts.
- **Seam:** *the Roost* — it perches high and sheds burning scales (random tiles) for 12 s while the party fights its tail/adds.
- **Finale:** the Heart Burn — a floor-wide fire that shrinks to a safe island the party picks by where the last scorch is.
- **Hard (three new moves):** *Wingbeat* (a knockback that follows its flight line), *Cinder Rain* (scattered embers each leaving a small red circle), *Heart Flare* (a stack on a random non-tank, then a spread).
- **Mood:** black basalt, orange glow, bone.
- **Music prompt direction:** war drums, low brass, a dragon-roar motif; two themes, one for ground and one soaring for flight.

### Boss VI — Hrimgar, the Frost Titan · "Warden of the White Reach"
- **Place:** a glacier plateau under aurora, with ice sheets and standing stones.
- **Shape:** *the slow giant, and the ground itself.* Huge, deliberate attacks; the **terrain** is the mechanic.
- **Question:** *what will the floor do?*
- **Acts (4):** the Thaw / the Freeze / the Crack / the Avalanche.
- **Unique set piece — the Ice Sheets:** the floor is a mosaic of sheets that **crack** (marked in advance) and sink; cracked sheets become water you can't stand on, frozen sheets are slippery (you slide a short way). The Titan's strikes cause the cracks, so *where it hits is where the floor leaves*.
- **Soak:** a random player is **Frostbitten** (a slow-building chill). They must be warmed at one of the braziers by walking there with the party stack, so a pair of players guards the route.
- **Seam:** the Long Night — the aurora dims, huge shapes loom; the Titan swings in slow, hugely telegraphed arcs for 20 s while the party reshapes the floor.
- **Finale:** the plateau tips and the avalanche runs the length of the arena in lanes; safe lanes open by where the sheets are frozen.
- **Hard:** *Glacial Stomp* (a ring of shards after a stomp), *Breath of Winter* (a cone that freezes a sheet instead of damaging, then a delayed burst), *Titan's Grip* (grabs a random player, who must be freed by a stack).
- **Mood:** pale blue, white, aurora green and violet.
- **Music prompt direction:** deep choir drone, glacial bells, giant footfalls; builds in layers of ice-crack percussion.

### Boss VII — Azerath, the Sunken Archmage · "Master of the Seven Circles"
- **Place:** a drowned tower whose floors are spell circles, each in a different element.
- **Shape:** *the rotating lab.* Each element changes what the arena does.
- **Question:** *which element is it, and who holds the rune?*
- **Acts (4 + a lull):** Fire / Frost / Lightning / Void; between each, the room reconfigures.
- **Unique set piece — the Rune Pair:** two random players are marked with runes of different elements. The runes pair into a **reaction** when the two stand near each other (fire + frost = steam cloud, etc.), so players choose to pair or split depending on what the next spell draws. The right choice changes by element.
- **Seam:** the Binding — Azerath calls a summoned elemental you must route to the matching circle (a position puzzle with random element order).
- **Finale:** all circles lit at once; the Archmage casts a **combined** spell where each circle's telegraph is its own element and a reaction shows in the overlap.
- **Hard:** *Counterspell* (a delayed copy of the last mechanic mirrored across the room), *Rune Swap* (the two marks swap bearers), *Arcane Mark* (a random player is stacked on by their own elemental).
- **Mood:** deep teal tower stone with elemental colours per act.
- **Music prompt direction:** arcane strings and choir, a motif that modulates by element, electric undertones.

### Boss VIII — The Hydra of the Drowned Temple · "Mother of Nine Heads"
- **Place:** a flooded temple, pillars, a stagnant pool.
- **Shape:** *many threats, one body.* The **heads** are the mechanic.
- **Question:** *which head is which, and what do they want?*
- **Acts (3):** Three heads / Six heads / Nine heads. Cutting a head grows two lesser ones unless the stump is **seared** (a fire step on the right pillar), so route choices matter.
- **Unique set piece — the Head Draw:** each head has its own element of attack (acid cone, tidal line, venom stack, gaze, etc.), and each one targets a **random player**. Heads are visible and numbered by colour; the party must decide *which head to kill first*, because the order changes what the next act brings.
- **Seam:** the Submerge — the Hydra dives for 15 s while the temple floods and the heads rise at random columns; the party fights the heads one by one (a targetable lull, not an idle one).
- **Finale:** the Mother's Heart — all heads at once, but the order they strike is set by the order the party killed them, so earlier choices come back at the end.
- **Hard:** *Regrowth* (a missed searing grows an extra head), *Tidal Dance* (two heads' lines cross with a safe wedge between them, never painted), *Hydra's Gaze* (a back-to-the-wall mechanic).
- **Mood:** algae green, brass, black water, a gold pool.
- **Music prompt direction:** drums and low choir with a head-count layer: each head adds an instrument.

### Boss IX (maybe) — The Void Gate · "The Door That Was Never Built"
Only if its mechanics are strong enough to earn the finale. It must not be "a bigger boss"; it is a *different kind of fight*, with these four mechanics at its core:
1. **The Fold:** the arena folds along a line (marked on the floor). Everything on one side is **superimposed** on the other, so a telegraph drawn on your half also affects your mirror position. Players must read the *pair* of positions.
2. **Echo of Place:** a random player is "anchored": the boss records where they stand and they must be **back at that spot** in 6 s (a ring marks it), while the rest of the party clears the way. A position you committed to earlier is the mechanic.
3. **Unwritten:** a random player's role is swapped for 20 s (the tank gets damage-taking, the healer gets the threat); the party must adapt on the fly with visible role icons.
4. **The Seams:** the arena has hidden-in-plain-sight "seam" tiles that, when two players stand on matching seams, **open a rift** that cancels a floor-wide hit. Finding the matching pair is a thinking puzzle, and the seam colours are on the floor from the start of the act.
- **Acts (5):** Door / Fold / Echo / Unwritten / Open. The final act braids all four, with an HP-gate wall until the final seam is opened.
- **Mood:** black glass, white cracks, absent colour.
- **Music prompt direction:** reversed strings and bass drones, choir sung backward, silence used as an instrument.
- **If the mechanics don't hold up in the prototype, drop it and let the Hydra be the finale.** The Hydra is the fallback finale: its end braids the order the party chose.

## Order, difficulty ladder and gear

- **Order:** Wyrm, Frost Titan, Archmage, Hydra, then the Void Gate if built.
- **Ladder on Hard (bots at mid gear):** V ~55%, VI ~50%, VII ~48%, VIII ~45%, IX ~40%. Normal at fresh gear ~90%.
- Set 2 drops its own tier (4-piece bonuses), stacking with set 1's.

## Decisions (user, 2026-10-22)

1. Build the four first (Wyrm, Frost Titan, Archmage, Hydra); the Void Gate is a possible later finale. Until then the Hydra is the set's finale.
2. HP-gate pushes and walls are for set 2 only (not retrofitted to set 1).
3. New classes are wanted alongside; the first to try is a scythe user (see the class notes).

## What it takes (rough)

Per boss, using the existing loop (script, tells, preview, journal, icons, music, sounds, bot QA, gear sweep): about as much work as one set 1 boss. The new systems (HP-bar ticks, pushes, walls) go first, tried on Grubnak.
