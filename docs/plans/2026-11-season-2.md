# Season 2: The Silver Road (spoiler-free plan)

*2026-11-11. A plan, nothing built. This file gives away no mechanics. The full design, with every move on Normal, Hard and Extreme, is in `2026-11-season-2-design.md`; don't open it if you want the fights to be a surprise.*

## What it is

Four new bosses, numbered XIII to XVI, that open once the Cinder Sovereign has fallen. They are the first set of a second season: sets 1 to 3, the Ultimate and the secret raid become "Season 1" on the Raid Board, and this set sits under "Season 2" with room for more after it.

## The story

The court's crowns were made of moon-silver, dug from the caves under its kitchen. The silver was never the valley's. It is the Moon's own road down to the world, laid before there were kings, and the kings dug it up. For an age the Ember under the square glowed just enough to hide the valley from her. The night after the Ember went out was the first truly dark night Emberfall has had, and the first night the Moon could see it.

Now everything made of her silver is being called home, and her household is coming down the road to fetch the rest. They are not cruel. The silver is theirs. But a great deal of the valley is made of it by now.

This picks up what the first four bosses planted and never explained: the moon-silver caves, the thread Skitterra stole from the moon, the moon going down in the Hollow King's window.

**The thread through the set:** every room is a stage further up the road, and Emberfall can be seen from each one, smaller every time. In the last room the whole world hangs in the sky over your heads.

## How it plays

- Every fight is 8 to 10 minutes, fully choreographed, with no hidden telegraphs, like the rest of the game.
- **Normal** runs one written order. **Hard** draws one of three dances a pull and adds five moves a boss that Normal never uses. **Extreme** is Hard plus three more mechanics of the boss's own, in one fixed order.
- Difficulty climbs from XIII to XVI.
- No new classes and no new systems.

## How it opens

- The set opens once the account has cleared the Ultimate (in a friend's game, once the host has).
- A new way out of town appears. I'm not saying where.
- Bosses open in order. Hard opens once all four have fallen on Normal, then boss by boss. Extreme opens for a boss once it falls on Hard. Hard+ modifiers work here too.

## What you get

- **Gear:** a new tier in the system as it is (same slots, same four rarities, one step up, two Mythics a boss, four new party bonuses). No crafting, no upgrading.
- **Not gear:** feats for every clear, a title each for the set on Normal, Hard and Extreme, four trophies, more gold a clear than set 3.
- **The town:** each boss's first defeat adds something. Not saying what.
- A one-page epilogue after the finale's first clear.

## What you would paint

Prompts will be written for you in `docs/art/`, as before: four boss icons, four VFX reference sheets, the new item icons, and music prompts for four boss tracks and a set theme. Until they are painted the game uses drawn placeholders. The set's palette is silver, black water and frost blue, with one warm colour only: the town's windows.

## Build order

1. **You approve this plan** (the setting, the names, the looks). It is cheap to change now and expensive later.
2. **Groundwork:** the new set on the Raid Board, the unlock, the gear tier, the test tools.
3. **Garrow first, start to finish:** script, bots, art, sound, journal, tuning, full check. You play him before the other three are built, and what you say shapes the rest.
4. **Vesper and Ormun**, built side by side.
5. **Maelis Orne** last.
6. **A pass over the whole set:** tuning so the finale is the hardest, gear, feats, town, story.
7. **Full check, you play it, and it ships when you say so.**

Each boss is about seven small agent tasks plus tuning. Nothing is committed or pushed without your word.
