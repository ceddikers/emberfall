# Code, boss-clarity and VFX audit (2026-10-19)

These findings come from read-only audits. Line numbers are as of commit 128aad1, so they drift as the code changes. Re-check each item before acting on it.

## A. Smoothness, performance, network, bugs (most impact first)

1. **SMOOTHNESS: interpolation runs on the capped game clock `now`.** It only advances by min(dt, 0.05), so a stalled frame makes `SendClock` jump and `Track` bunches samples 1 ms apart. That causes overshoot and snap-back of up to ±20 px a frame.
   - Fix: use `performance.now()/1000` for send stamps, arrival times and render time. Keep `now` for gameplay.
   - In `Track.at()`, use a minimum gap of 0.05 and cap extrapolation. Drop samples that map backwards.
   - Where: main.js send stamps around 202/509/1313, arrivals around 811/903/1100/2250/2273, render around 1323/1394/1425; interp.js 40/54.
2. **BUG: background tabs pile up and replay.** Tracks never trim while rAF is paused, so a hidden tab ends up with 600+ samples and fast-forwards on return. FX/VFX lists and the main `timers` queue up and fire all at once.
   - Fix: cap `Track.s` at about 32.
   - On `visibilitychange`: skip cosmetic spawns while hidden. On return, clear FX/VFX, empty `timers` and reset tracks to their latest sample.
3. **PERF: `ctx.filter` on the boss every frame** (Long Night brightness; the trial Shade filter with drop-shadow). Fix: draw the boss into an offscreen canvas once, then `drawImage` it with the filter.
4. **PERF: DOM writes every frame.** raid-ui boss bar text, the countdown and party-frame widths; hud.js slot text and `--cd` on 7 elements. Fix: cache the elements, write only on change, and use `transform: scaleX` for bars.
5. **PERF: `glow()` creates a radial gradient per call.** About 95 per frame in barrowLife, plus kitchen lamps, peak clouds, flames and puddles. Fix: cached per-colour sprites with the 2-stop falloff.
6. **PERF: spawn rates depend on frame rate.** Affected: dash trail ghosts (a full `drawHero` each), missile trails, VFX ticks and ability-vfx 287. Fix: fixed-rate spawning (dt accumulators).
7. **NET:** the state stream is ordered and reliable (net.js 240), so one lost packet stalls `snap`/`fb`/`bots`/`adds`. Fix: add a second unordered/unreliable channel for latest-wins messages.
8. **BUG: pressing abilities during the countdown spends the cooldown, but the effect is dropped.** `sendCombat` and the sim only accept events in 'live'. Fix: block presses in the countdown (except dash), or accept non-damage events.
9. **NET/FAIRNESS: guests see telegraphs one round trip late.** Fix: shift the client start back by rtt/2, or carry the fight clock in `tg`/`roll`/`sweep`.
10. **PERF: `rgba()`/`shade()` parse hex and allocate a string on every call.** Fix: memoise them.
11. **PERF: the arena floor is rebuilt on every resize** (the scale key changes continuously). Fix: round the scale to 0.25 steps and build it in `enterArena` behind the veil.
12. **PERF (host): `players()`/`alive()` allocate 10-20 times per tick, and `view()` is rebuilt every tick.** Fix: build both once per tick, and build `view()` only when a bot thinks.
13. **NET: many small messages** (`fb`, `adds`, `bots`, `fps`, plus `snap` relayed across scenes). Fix: merge them into one `tick` message, and don't relay `snap` to players in the other scene.
14. **BUG: `cev` hot/partyBuff values are not range-checked.** A bad hot can freeze the host. Fix: clamp ticks ≤ 20 and interval ≥ 0.5; whitelist buff kinds, cap value at 0.1 and duration at 30.
15. **BUG (race): a stale `rleave` can hit the next raid.** Fix: add a raid sequence id to `raid`, `rleave` and `cev`.
16. **PERF: town has no off-screen culling.** Fix: a bounding-box check with about 150 px margin.
17. **PERF: particles each do save/translate/rotate/restore and switch blend mode.** Fix: use `setTransform`, and batch draws by blend mode.
18. **BUG: the main `timers` survive a scene change.** Fix: clear them in `enterArena`/`leaveRaid`.
19. **BUG/leak:** the host's timeout worker is never stopped, and blob URLs are never revoked (net.js 17/197).
20. **SMOOTHNESS:** the town camera rounds to whole pixels (main.js about 1609), so the player jitters. Camera smoothing uses `min(1, dt*k)`; use `1-exp(-k*dt)` instead.
21. **BUG:** `bot.taken` is never trimmed (raid-host 141/210).
22. **PERF:** DPR 2 means 4× the pixels. Add a render-scale option or auto-drop when frames go over 20 ms.
23. **Small items:** rooted turning uses a fixed 0.016 instead of dt; `areaWarning` animates on `performance.now()` instead of `now`.

## B. Boss clarity and VFX: the top 15

1. **Fill sweep, late pulse and lock flash on every boss tell** (boss-tells.js `outline()`/`ground()`). `ArenaView.areaWarning` has the fill and pulse, but raids never use it. Draw `ArenaView.grown(s,p)` at about 0.12 alpha with a bright leading edge, add a solid pulsing rim in the last 0.6 s, and flash white when a shape locks (`tg.lockAt`). Port areaWarning's additive 9 px edge glow.
2. **Telegraphs sit under floor VFX** (drawTelegraphs runs before `FX.draw`/`VFX.draw('ground')`). Add a rim-only redraw pass after the ground VFX.
3. **Eightfold Lunge.** Its dark purple fill disappears on the dark floor, and two pairs are on screen at once. Fix: pink fill and rim, the next strike shown bright with the sweep, later strikes as ghosts with order pips. Its impact is weak; add spikeburst, debris and shake(4) per step.
4. **Whisk crosses overlap.** Show the next one bright, the following one as a ghost, and add a rotation-direction arrow. `f.ct(3) - 0.4 + 0.4` at grubnak.js 141 does nothing.
5. **`VFX.slash`** is thin and additive-only. Add a source-over ink band, a trailing smear and a tip glint. This upgrades about 15 melee abilities and the auto-attacks.
6. **Cache `glow()` as a sprite** (see A5).
7. **Mince** fades out on the far half of the room. Use a uniform 0.15 tint plus a sweep, with debris along the chop and a spikeburst at the edge.
8. **Failed mechanics look like successes.** The host adds `fail:1` to `tgx` for towers, plates, rods, bells and stacks. The client plays a red shapeFlash, a red roomTint and shake(8). A Barrow Bells fail gets a big purple moment.
9. **Hit feedback:** a red edge vignette for 0.35 s when you take damage. Make shake directional and decay more slowly on heavy hits.
10. **Donut safe spots** (Lid Slam, Crown of Graves): tint the danger area outside r2, and give the safe circle a bright rim and a soft glow.
11. **Colour language.** The rim colour comes from the role: orange-red damage, yellow stack, purple spread, cyan soak, red buster. Theme colour goes on the interior only. Hollow King swings currently use a mint rim, which reads as safe.
12. **Royal Gaze impact:** add a flash at the eye, a beam to each caught player, stone crystals on them and shake(5).
13. **Fireball:** use a teardrop flame head, 4-6 embers a frame with drag, and a shock ring on hit. Affects fireball, pyro and condemn.
14. **Weak player abilities:**
    - `KIT_VFX.dash`: dust swirl plus speed streaks.
    - Backstab: converging crimson streaks plus a spikeburst.
    - `runeguard.strike`: add a slash.
    - Smite: a mini lightfall.
    - `ranger.quick`: sonic rings.
    - Venom: drips.
    - Barrage and shift: a pillar or ribbon.
15. **Particle engine:** `parts.shift()` and `splice` are O(n); use swap-remove or compaction. Spawning depends on frame rate.

### Per-boss notes

- **Grubnak:**
  - Lid Slam's danger area is untinted.
  - The Simmer rings have no shake.
  - Meatballs need rolling dust and a wall impact.
  - Kitchen Rush needs a speed streak and debris.
  - Gorge needs its own tag.
- **Skitterra:**
  - Silk Wrap should leave a cocoon on the tank during the root.
  - Venom Spit should fire a travelling glob at resolve.
  - The Brood's 5 s "kill the other" deadline isn't shown: add a countdown ring on the survivor.
  - Silk Reel needs a reticle on the hooked player during the windup.
- **Volkar:** the most readable fight.
  - Gale Slam needs a wall-impact puff.
  - Storm Walls need a spark burst when they clip a player.
- **Hollow King:**
  - Swing rims read as safe.
  - Crown of Graves' safe circle isn't highlighted.
  - Gaze impact (see B12).
  - Doom expiry should be a purple execution pillar.
  - Bells fail moment (see B8).
  - Long Night rims need to be brighter.
- **Hard drops outlines on every tell** (`!env.hard`, boss-tells 696), not just the hidden ones. Consider keeping a faint solid edge on tells that aren't hidden.
