# The life pass (2026-11-08)

The owner: "the game is fun but a few glaring gameplay issues feel off and I can't put my finger on why", and it is "missing a touch of life". Eleven read-only critiques (control feel, the host sim, bots, the three boss sets, the three roles' kits, the loop around a pull, sound and effects) went through the whole game. This is what they found, what was changed on branch `life`, and what was left for the owner to decide.

## What felt off, and why

1. **One slip was a death, and the second system made sure of it.** The 2026-10-31 retune (7ced305) tuned every boss against bots holding talents and added a per-boss Normal multiplier. After it, most avoidable hits on Normal were 85 to 150% of a DPS's health bar on all twelve bosses (Grubnak 49 of 64 avoidable shapes at 85% or more, the Hollow King's swings 97%, set 2 and 3 much the same). The design doc says "well over half; two usually kill". Vulnerability (+50% a stack) then multiplied even unavoidable raidwides, so a raidwide that was 59% became 89%, and a raised player came back at 50% health still carrying the Vulnerability that killed them. Recovering exists only on Hard. Closing passes added 10% boss damage per pass for the rest of the fight. The feeling: there is no small mistake, and a death snowballs.
2. **Phantom hits.** A Meat Toss puddle formed under the marked player's feet and ticked on the same frame, so a correctly spread hero took an "avoidable" hit with Damage Down (9 to 19 of them per bot run). Zones and fires did the same. A boss auto landing just before a tankbuster consumed the Bastion's Perfect Block or the Ironpalm's Read (about one buster in five at a normal reaction), so the buster landed bare. A guest's dash was judged from where it started (`e.tx = msg.x`). A pushed act started its first cast on top of a running tankbuster.
3. **Inputs that looked accepted and did nothing.** A GCD press more than half a second early flashed the slot and was dropped with no feedback (the queue window, 0.5 s, was shorter than the 0.6 s animation lock). A cast stopped held right-click steering until the button was released and pressed again. A heal re-resolved its target when the cast ended, so a cursor that wandered moved the heal, and with no hover or lock a heal defaulted to yourself (the cursor is on the floor when you move by right-click). The nearest-to-cursor fallback could turn a main heal into a 5 s raise cast. Glows lit while the GCD still had two seconds to run.
4. **The party was three perfect, silent clones.** Bots dodged on the frame a telegraph appeared and never died, so the human was the only one who ever failed. Lines exist for all 23 heroes but were gated to the local hero. The same four names and classes every pull, standing on a fixed grid that ignored where the human stood, raising a corpse the instant the raise was ready.
5. **The spaces between the big moments were silent.** No footsteps or dust in the arena, no sound on your own hits landing (only at the press, so a projectile thudded before it arrived), the boss's swing silent, no heartbeat beyond a sound under 25% health, heroes and bosses standing as statics. A missing music id (the pull, the kill and wipe stings, all of set 3 and the Ultimate) faded the music to silence instead of keeping what was playing.
6. **The loop around a pull.** A kill got 3.4 s before a full-screen blurred card nobody could dismiss, with the chest unreachable behind it and Try again below the fold. A retry replayed the whole entrance and then waited for someone to find a small countdown button. Town had no memory of the pull.
7. **Script timing.** Several locks were unwalkable at their range (Venom Spit at 1.2 s, Ember Breath at 1.1 s, Comet Draw at 0.3 s). The Frost Titan's seam collided with its next set at human kill paces. The Hydra's heads cut the single cast bar 85 times a fight. The Hollow King's Court had a Knell on Normal that Hard had already removed as too much. Set 3's Chorus painted about fifteen shapes at one instant.

## What changed (branch `life`)

**Host rules (`fight.js`).** On Normal a single avoidable hit that is not a buster, a fail, a fall, a bill or the enrage is capped at 80% of the victim's max health before cuts (`FIGHT.NORMAL_SLIP_CAP`); Vulnerability still lands on top, so a second slip inside it kills. Vulnerability never multiplies an unavoidable raidwide on Normal and counts one stack at most on Hard. A raise clears Vulnerability, Damage Down, slows and roots. A puddle, zone or fire that forms under a hero gives a full beat before its first tick, and stepping into one gives half a second. The boss's auto is held from 1 s before a tankbuster on that tank until 0.3 s after. A pushed act's first step waits for a running cast bar (the act itself begins on the gate, so fight length is unchanged). What closing passes piled on is halved when the next act begins. The Limit Break's clean-mechanic gain is 6 (was 4). The boss follows its tank's steps at 6 rad/s instead of snapping (a swing past about 50 degrees, such as a taunt swap, still snaps, and the Hunting Horn turns him at once). Hard, Hard+, trials, PvP and the Ultimate are untouched by the cap.

**Control (`main.js`, `engine.js`, `hud.js`, `kits.js`).** Held right-click steering resumes when a cast ends or is interrupted. A press the engine drops shakes the slot instead of lighting it; the queue window is 0.7 s. An ally cast keeps the target it started on while they live. With no hover or lock a heal goes to the most hurt living ally in range (yourself only when everyone is full); a raise needs a hover, a frame or a lock. A glow is full only when the press would fire within 0.35 s with a target in reach, dimmed otherwise. Melee reach 105 past the boss edge (was 75). Eviscerate's positional counts the flank or the rear. A hairline on the cast bar marks where moving stops cancelling.

**Bots (`bots.js`, `raid-host.js`, `voice-lines.js`).** Bots speak through their own classes' lines (down, raised, low, Ultimate, victory, and a new line per healer when a raise starts), captions only, as a murmur: a low chance each (an Ultimate 25%, a death 50%), at least 25 s between two bot lines, at most six a pull, none in the first 10 s of a pull or on the heels of a boss line; the raise line and the victory line are outside the gap and the cap. Their captions are drawn smaller and dimmer and never push a boss or hero line out. In a measured 69 s pull with several bot deaths and Ultimates, one bot line showed against three boss lines. The local hero's right-click bark is now 60% on a 20 s cooldown (was every 9 s), and town idle chatter comes every minute or two. The fill-in damage bots play a little like people (`BotHuman.LIVELY`: a 0.25 to 0.45 s look at the floor, now and then a late dodge or a lost moment, never the wrong way); tank and healer stay exact. A healer notices a fall 1.2 to 2 s before the raise. Ranged and healer bots take the side away from a human and step off a human within 60 px. Each class has its own name (Ilsa is always the Cleric); the party is drawn at random within each role's three classes, and a retry keeps the same party. After a clear the bots wave.

**Life (`audio.js`, `sound-hooks.js`, `sfx-synth.js`, `heroes.js`, `boss-vfx.js`, `main.js`).** A music id with no file keeps the track that is playing (the kill and wipe muffle the room for 3 s instead); set 3 and the Ultimate stand in on the set 2 track in the same slot until their files land (`MUSIC_STAND_IN`, to be removed then). Footsteps and dust in the arena, yours and everyone's, with a surface per room, and a boss footfall every other stride with a 2 px settle. Your own hits land with a quiet confirm at the enemy, pitched lower the bigger the hit; a crit above your usual gets a 2 px shake and a beat of stop. Chip and ticks no longer flush the screen red; a hit over 60% of your health dips the music for a beat and stops the camera for 0.04 s. Under 30% health the screen's edge holds a slow red pulse and the music dulls to 4 kHz. The boss's swing is heard; a telegraph landing on nobody still sounds. Heroes breathe when standing and bosses breathe slowly. A low bell and a swell at the 60 s enrage warning, and a soft tick a second for the last ten before the cast. New recipes: `hit-confirm`, `boss-step`, `boss-auto`, `boss-impact-empty`, `enrage-bell`.

**The loop (`raid-ui.js`, `loot.js`, `town-life.js`, `town-npcs.js`, `whats-new.js`, `style.css`).** A clear waits 6 s before the results card; Esc or Hide drops the card to a slim strip and unblurs the room so the chest is in reach; Try again is sticky and Enter presses it for the host. A retry skips the entrance (name plate only, music plays on) and the host's pull countdown starts by itself, cancelled by the clock or Esc. The chest's cards land one by one with a rarity sweep, Legendary and Mythic with a ring and a rising cue; the host's Try again or Town asks "Leave N items?" once when another human still has pieces to choose. Hask remarks on the pull the party just came back from (six lines per outcome, a wipe streak counted); idle townsfolk say one of their own lines now and then, never one addressed at the player. What's New has the entries since 2026-10-25.

**Scripts.** Set 1: Flambé stripes 1.05 s apart on Normal; Normal plate fail 9000 (was 16100); Normal grease puddles 16 s and stains 30 s; Venom Spit locks at 1.8 s (Hard 1.5) with its pool 180 px beyond the target and a lighter Normal tick; the Dark Between's stack lands 5 s before the next drop; the Court's Normal Knell is a Sceptre Throw as on Hard; the Long Night's closing pass has no Aftershock on Normal. Set 2: the Ember Heart's island shows 2.2 s earlier and the Roar no longer cuts the Heart Burn bar (Last Ember's two sets sit 3.5 s closer to pay for it); Ember Breath locks at 1.6 s (Hard 1.3); the Frost Titan's Long Night no longer collides with the next set (0 overlaps at every kill pace from 440 to 520 s), and Normal's Avalanche act calls its Howl two sets earlier so raidwides stay inside 150 s; Normal black water ticks about 13% of a DPS's health a second (was 27%); the Hydra's heads keep 4.8 s between casts on Normal (3.8 on Hard), the body guard checks both Thrash rings, and the Undertow gap is 1.8 s on Normal. Set 3: an unwalkable lock is now 0.55 s on Normal (0.45 Hard); a glass zone ticks for 3500 (Hard 5200, was 9200 / 9600); a raidwide moved 9 s clear of a tankbuster pair; a lane plan keeps clear of a rooted hero; the finale's guest shapes appear 3.6 s before their own hit instead of all at once; the next cut of a dance is the bright one (floor 0.5); 4 s more between a party-wide drop to 1 HP and the next tankbuster on Normal.

## The VFX pass (same day, after the owner's "cooler, not distracting")

The gallery's contact sheets (`tools/vfx-gallery`) put the game's effects beside the painted sheets in `docs/art/vfx-reference/`. The effects were clean and never cluttered the floor, but thin and small: at the game's zoom a hero is about 40 world px and most climaxes were hero-sized. The painted grammar is a small wind-up, one bold climax shape (hot white core, saturated body, dark ink edge), then a slow aftermath.

- **One primitive, `VFX.climax`** (vfx.js): a bold silhouette (burst, disc, ring, crescent, bolt, cone or cross) with ink edge, body and core; it pops in over 0.07 s with a little overshoot, holds at full for about 0.1 s, then dissipates with the ink fading faster than the light; an aftermath of smoke, embers and a floor mark under 0.3 alpha and under 2 s.
- **Rules applied to all 23 classes** (seven agents, one patch each, applied to one file): only cooldowns of 60 s or more, combo finishers, signature moments (a Perfect Block, Hot Streak, Keen Eye, a Perfect Deflect, Thermal Shock...) and Ultimates got the bold climax, at least twice the hero (r 80 or more); fillers are untouched; each class keeps its palette and props; no new full-screen effect; a few effects that were over the 120-particle budget came down (Supernova 129 to 95, Grand Loom 142 to 104, the Finale's stars 172 to 114, the Alembist's vial reaction 138 to 78).
- **Checked:** `render.js all-classes --check` passes every effect (0 failing in all 23 classes). Class peaks are mostly 30 to 80 particles; Ranger (Starfall), Pyromancer and Cleric (Dawn Breaks) still peak near 140 from effects that were already that size.
- **Caveats:** the three big tank Ultimates' ground bursts (r 140 to 185) are solid for about 0.2 s before they fade, as the spikebursts they replace were; the Marionettist's Grand Finale fifth beat and a few hound and lantern paths are not exercised by the gallery; several agents judged their classes by the check and the particle numbers rather than by eye. Worth a look in play.

## Left for the owner

- **Set 3 Normal is over the target for human-like bots** (24/24 on Corvane and Caelum) and was before this pass. The per-hit numbers from the 10-31 retune are untouched here; whether to bring Normal damage down per boss, or raise what the bots take, is a tuning call.
- **Grubnak Hard at mid gear came out 2/4** for perfect bots in one small sample (target 60 to 95%). Worth 48 runs before concluding anything.
- **The Hydra's body moves and head casts still cut each other's bars** about 60 times a fight (`bodyAt` waits for earlier body casts, not a running head cast). Fixing it shifts the whole fight's timing.
- **Caelum's Wake ring points sit at a fixed radius**, so the rim is always safe; a design change if wanted.
- **Guests are still judged on positions up to about 200 ms old**, with a 14 px pad; a small lag allowance for remote humans (judge them about 0.1 s after the resolve) would make a two-machine party fairer. Not done here.
- **Arena room tone** (a quiet bed per room under the music) needs new loop recipes.
- **Music files** for set 3, the Ultimate, the pull, victory and wipe stings; the stand-in table in `audio.js` goes when they land.
- **Teammates' lines.** Another player's fall, return, Ultimate and low health now show as the same small captions as a bot's, on the same budget (before, only the local hero and bots ever spoke).
- **VFX gallery:** `render.js grubnak --check` fails on `tg:skewer/buster` with a NaN `moveTo` in draw-util.js. It fails the same way on `main`, so it predates this branch.
- **Melee reach for bots** stays at 75 in `raid-host.js` and `pvp-bots.js` so the boss tuning baselines hold; raising it to 105 means a re-tune.
- The balance simulator (`tools/simulate.js`) models raidwides at half their real size, takes melee out of range only for newbies, and cannot see any of the input problems above. `bot-raid.js HUMAN=dps` per class is the better yardstick.

## Follow-up (2026-11-08, after 17b4787 went live)

The healer bot's raise delay (`BotAI.noticed`, 1.2 to 2 s after a fall) restarted its timer whenever more than 1.5 s passed between asks, so a healer busy in a heavy act could put the raise off for good. The Ultimate felt it most: the human-like party's per-act clears went from 96/75/46/38 (f760e25) to 83/29/33/8, with Act II taking a minute longer. The timer now runs once from the first ask and holds while the hero stays down, forgotten when they are seen alive again. Re-measured: 92/88/54/29 on 24 runs per act, inside the usual seed swing of the baseline. Set 1 to 3 clears were unchanged either way (24/24 on set 3 Normal before and after).
