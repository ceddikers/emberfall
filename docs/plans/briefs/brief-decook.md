# De-cook pass brief (one agent per boss)

Game: Emberfall, a browser raid game at C:\Users\Cedef\OneDrive\Documents\newgame. Read CLAUDE.md first (design rules, commands, line endings). Several agents work at once, one boss each, on their own files.

## Why
The user played Queen Skitterra and found almost no time to damage her between mechanics. Four passes in one day (2026-10-26: pairs of "a move and the one that answers it", "Act II as dense as Act I", filling every tank/healer gap, new tank moves) each added density, and the stacking of two moves into each other became the default rhythm instead of a highlight. In the user's words it got "overtuned and overused to the point it isn't tasteful anymore". Every fight gets a de-cook pass; the busiest need the most.

## Goal for your boss
- **Pairs are seasoning.** Keep a pair (two moves stacked or back to back as one problem) only where it is a signature moment of the fight: about two or three per act at most, fewer is fine. Elsewhere a move stands on its own with room after it. Prefer removing a use of a move over removing a move from the fight (every cast name should still appear somewhere, or journal-lint fails; if you do drop a cast name entirely, remove its journal entries too and tell me its sound name so I can clean up).
- **Damage windows.** Measured with the uptime harness (below) on Normal, bots: melee DPS on the boss at least ~80% (adds that need killing count as useful), ranged ~90%, and at least one quiet window of 6 s or more (no telegraph live, boss targetable) about every 40 s, in every act. Bots dodge perfectly, so humans will sit well below these; err toward more room, not less. Hard may be denser than Normal, but by layers and sharper reads, not by removing every breather: aim melee ≥ ~72% and a quiet window about every 50 s.
- **Keep what matters:** the fight's own idea and its signature moments (read DESIGN.md's answers for your boss and the plan doc), tank and healer beats (the tank's longest wait about 25-30 s, the 2026-10-26 tank move stays; the healer keeps something every ~30 s, light beats are fine), the ending peaks (the last act may be the densest, but still with windows), Hard's per-pull draws (update their limits for the new order; no fallback to a fixed order), Hard keeps 3 to ~6 moves Normal never uses, Hard ≥ 1.1x Normal on every shared move, no warning under 1.2 s, no cast cutting another's bar.
- **Timeline rules** (check.js timeline test): script 480-600 s with every set run, the upcoming feed always three named steps ahead, no gap over 16 s with nothing named, a raidwide at least every 150 s and a tankbuster every 180 s. A quieter fight still has to reach 8-10 minutes: spread the moves out (longer calms), don't pad with filler casts.
- **Retune** afterwards: more time on the boss means faster kills, so bring the bot clear rates back into band (HP or the knobs your file has).
